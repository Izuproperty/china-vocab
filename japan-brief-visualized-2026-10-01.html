STORY 1 · AI & TECH
OpenAI Seeks a $1.4 Trillion Valuation on the Day SoftBank's Last $10 Billion Goes In

OpenAI opened talks on a raise of at least $30 billion at about $1.4 trillion pre-money, after putting its IPO on hold. The March round was priced at $852 billion post-money. SoftBank Group rose 6.2% on September 30.


SoftBank's final $10 billion tranche is due today, funded by a record high-yield bond with dollar coupons up to 9.75%. That brings its commitment to about $64.6 billion for roughly 13%, worth about $180 billion at the new price before dilution.

Investor takeaway: A higher OpenAI mark widens SoftBank's borrowing room under its loan-to-value ceiling, but the gain is unrealized while its coupons are paid in cash; the round's closing price and SoftBank's November results are the checkpoints.

STORY 2 · SEMICONDUCTORS & COMPONENTS
TDK and Taiyo Yuden Open Alliance Talks to Build Capacitors for AI Servers Together

The two signed an MOU on September 29 covering joint development of high-capacitance MLCCs and inductors for AI data centers and physical AI, cooperation in manufacturing and procurement, and a possible capital alliance. They may seek joint METI approval under the Economic Security Promotion Act.


AI accelerators need dense banks of capacitors and inductors to keep power stable, and the high-capacitance parts are the hardest to make. Murata and Samsung Electro-Mechanics lead the high end; Chinese makers are growing in standard grades.

Investor takeaway: Pooled procurement and R&D help the number three and four players keep pace on AI-grade parts; a capital tie-up with a figure attached would make them a combined challenger to Murata, and nothing in the MOU commits them to one.

STORY 3 · MACRO & BOJ
Factory Output Drops 1.7% in August as Car Production Slides, With the Tankan Out This Morning

METI's preliminary industrial production fell 1.7% in August against a forecast 1.7% rise, the second straight decline. Motor vehicles fell 6.8%. Retail sales rose 2.7% year on year, below the roughly 3.3% expected.


Manufacturers project gains of 3.2% in September and 3.1% in October. The Tankan is out at 8:50 JST today, with 12 think tanks expecting the large-manufacturer index at +26, up from +22 in June on AI and chip demand.

Investor takeaway: A Tankan at or above +26 would let the BOJ read August as an auto-led blip; a miss on top of this data would cut October hike odds, already put at 20% to 30%, and weigh on bank stocks.
