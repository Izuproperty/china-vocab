STORY 1 · AI & TECH · FOUNDATION MODELS
Alibaba Launches Qwen4-Max as a Closed Model at $1.50 per Million Input Tokens, and Independent Tests Place It Just Behind Kimi K3.1 Pro

Alibaba (阿里巴巴) launched Qwen4-Max on Nov 16: 2.4T total and 71B active parameters, a 1M-token context window (上下文窗口), and no open weights. Input costs $1.50 per million tokens in Singapore and $1.413 in Beijing.


Independent scores trail the vendor charts. Artificial Analysis gives it 43 against 46 for Moonshot's Kimi K3.1 Pro, and Vals has the two models level at about 63%. The move to closed-source (闭源) reverses the August open release of Qwen3.8-Max.

Key signal: Alibaba now sells its best model through Alibaba Cloud instead of publishing it, and is cutting prices to offset independent scores just below Kimi's.

STORY 2 · AI & TECH · CHIPS / EXPORT CONTROLS
Washington Requires Licences for AI Chips Sent to Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand, Indonesia and Brazil, Targeting the Data Centres Chinese Labs Rent, Three Days Before Trump Meets Xi in Shenzhen

BIS (工业与安全局) now requires a licence (许可证) for advanced AI chips sent to five countries and for overseas data centres serving Chinese companies. The rule takes effect in 90 days and targets remote access (远程访问) to US chips.


It follows Jensen Huang's Nov 12 resignation over links to Megaspeed. The Institute for Progress estimates Chinese firms rent about 1M H100-equivalents offshore, rising to 2.8M by end-2027.

Key signal: The rental route to Nvidia chips narrows after a 90-day window, pushing more Chinese demand toward Huawei Ascend and T-Head.

STORY 3 · FINANCE · MACRO DATA
October Data Misses on Every Headline: Retail Sales Rise 0.4%, Industrial Output 4.8%, and Fixed-Asset Investment Falls 8.1% for the Year to Date

October retail sales (社会消费品零售总额) rose 0.4%, industrial output 4.8%, and fixed-asset investment (固定资产投资) fell 8.1% year to date. All three missed the Reuters poll, and property investment fell 21.6%.


High-tech manufacturing grew 17.6% and exports 28.7% in yuan terms. The NBS described strong supply, weak demand (供强需弱).

Key signal: Exports and high-tech output are carrying growth while households and investors pull back, so December's policy meeting carries more weight.
