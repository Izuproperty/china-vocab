STORY 1 · AI & TECH
DeepSeek closes $7.4B round at $74B valuation, discloses 82.9% API margins ahead of 2027 IPO push

DeepSeek is finalizing a ~RMB 50B (~$7.4B) round at a ~$74B valuation, closing by end of August, with every disclosed investor across both rounds mainland-based. First public financials show H1 revenue ~10x full-year 2025, narrowing losses, and a striking 82.9% API gross margin — set right after an August price hike on V4-Pro.


The margin number is a post-hike snapshot, not a steady-state figure. Whether it survives further undercutting from Qwen and Kimi K3 will matter more for positioning than the IPO prep itself.

Key signal: An all-domestic cap table plus a fresh price hike that stuck — pricing power is the story, not just the valuation headline.

STORY 2 · AI & TECH
Tencent open-sources 770B-parameter Hy4 Preview as capex surges 176% into first-ever negative free cash flow

Hy4 Preview (770B total / 49B active params, 1M-token context) is Tencent Hunyuan's first release under ex-OpenAI researcher Yao Shunyu. Tencent's own blind evaluation shows a narrow edge over GLM-5.3 and Kimi K3 — not an outright lead, and self-administered.


The same week, Tencent disclosed Q2 capex up 176% y/y, pushing free cash flow negative for the first time despite RMB 100B+ in annual profit. The question is whether thin benchmark gains justify a near-tripled compute spend.

Key signal: The office-agent race (WorkBuddy's 11.15M MAU vs. Alibaba's Qianwen Work, ByteDance's Doubao Work) may be the more relevant scoreboard than model benchmarks right now.

STORY 3 · FINANCE & MACRO
ICBC posts 3.3% first-half profit rise as policy lending lifts China's state banks

ICBC's H1 2026 net profit reached RMB 173.7B, up 3.3% y/y, continuing a pattern across the Big Four attributed to loan growth into infrastructure and policy-backed sectors as Beijing directs state-bank credit toward NDRC priorities.


The rise is modest against Q2 GDP growth that slowed to 4.3%, a three-year low. It arrives as Beijing signals new coordinated fiscal-and-financial policy for H2 2026 — bank earnings alone don't resolve whether real credit demand is recovering or policy lending is substituting for it.

Key signal: Policy-lending volumes at the Big Four are a rough proxy for how far Beijing leans on directed credit versus outright fiscal stimulus in H2.
