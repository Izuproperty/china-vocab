STORY 1 · AI & TECH · CHIPS
ByteDance, Alibaba and Tencent Order More Than 3 Million Nvidia RTX PRO 5500 Cards Worth Over $35 Billion, and Nvidia Says Partners May Get 5% to 10%

Reuters reported on October 20 that the three platforms ordered more than 3 million RTX PRO 5500 workstation cards, mainly for inference, and that Chinese orders overall top 6 million. The card performs about like the discontinued RTX 6000D and sits below the licence-restricted H200.


Nvidia says it can build no more than 3 million worldwide in 2027 as TSMC capacity shifts to Rubin, and partners may receive 5% to 10% of what they asked for. Samples arrive in December, volume deliveries from Q1 2027.

Key signal: Chinese platforms still want Nvidia for inference, but at a 5% to 10% fill rate most of the capacity they planned must come from Ascend, Cambricon, T-Head or rented cloud, and the orders give Washington a reason to look at workstation-class export rules.

STORY 2 · AI & TECH · DATA POLICY
Beijing Launches China National Data Group in Xiong'an, a Central SOE Owned by the State Council, Three Telecom Carriers and Both Grid Operators

Vice Premier Ding Xuexiang inaugurated the group on October 20. SASAC holds the owner's rights, a former China Unicom general manager chairs it, and a former National Data Administration deputy director runs it. SASAC lists it 28th among central SOEs.


Its mandate is to develop data resources and serve AI. The carriers bring networks and data centers, the grids bring power and industrial data. No capital figure, asset plan or pricing rule for public data has been published.

Key signal: Beijing now has one central counterparty for public and utility data, which could release scarce Chinese-language training data or ration it, and the listed carriers face possible asset transfers on undisclosed terms.

STORY 3 · FINANCE & MACRO
Lan Fo'an Opens the First APEC Finance Ministers' Meeting in Hong Kong With a Warning on Policy Spillovers, as the LPR Holds for a 17th Month

Lan told delegates from 21 economies to guard against negative spillovers from policy changes, cooperate on AI and keep supply chains open. He cited 4.6% nine-month growth and pledged to lift consumption without naming a new tool.


The LPR stayed at 3.0% and 3.5% the same day. The ¥1.3T ultra-long special bond program finished on October 16, about ¥1.45T of local bonds remain, and the ministry sells ¥15.5B of yuan bonds in Hong Kong on October 23.

Key signal: With rates on hold and the central special-bond program spent, fourth-quarter support depends on local bond sales and quota releases, and Lan's consumption pledge came without a new instrument.
