Sovereign AI Conflict
SoftBank is simultaneously the anchor investor in Japan's Noetra sovereign AI consortium and OpenAI's largest external shareholder — two entities on a competitive trajectory in physical AI.
Valuation Dependency

Tankan Inflation Signal
Firms raised 1-year CPI expectations to 2.7% (from 2.6%) and 5-year expectations to 2.6% — highest in data back to 2014. This reinforces BOJ board member Tamura's neutral rate estimate of ~2%.
Yen Paradox
Stronger Tankan data implying earlier BOJ hikes should theoretically support the yen — but USD/JPY hit 162.78 anyway. The 250-275bp Fed-BOJ differential overwhelms marginal BOJ guidance shifts.
Intervention Watch
Japan's Ministry of Finance spent ¥11.7 trillion ($73B) on FX intervention April-May with temporary effect. MoF reiterated readiness to act July 1, but verbal warnings are now read as floor signals rather than triggers.
