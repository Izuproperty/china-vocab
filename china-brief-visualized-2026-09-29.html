STORY 1 · AI & TECH
Beijing Asks ByteDance and Alibaba How Many Nvidia RTX Pro 5500s They Want, and Signals It Will Approve

MIIT asked ByteDance and Alibaba for purchase plans for Nvidia's RTX Pro 5500 and signalled approval, The Information reported on September 27. No volume, timing or official confirmation followed.


The Blackwell workstation card carries 84GB of GDDR7 and about 1.4 TB/s of bandwidth, enough for inference but well short of the H200 for training. A year ago Beijing told the same two firms to cancel RTX Pro 6000D orders.

Key signal: Beijing is opening imports for inference-grade parts only. The cleared volume decides whether Nvidia or Cambricon and Huawei gain inference share.

STORY 2 · AI & TECH
Kyland Swaps Out Linux, ROS and EtherCAT Inside a Robot, Then Lets a State Lab Try to Break It

Kyland Technology showed an embodied AI robot in Yichang on September 28 running its Intewell real-time OS, AUTBUS network bus and MaVIEW tools with an unnamed domestic AI chip.


An MIIT-affiliated security lab hit it and a conventional robot with joint damage, network attacks and virus injection; state media said only Kyland's kept running. China already buys 59% of the world's industrial robots, and domestic brands hold 55% of that market.

Key signal: Localization in robotics is moving from motors and reducers to the control software and buses. A named humanoid customer is still missing.

STORY 3 · FINANCE & MACRO
August Industrial Profits Rise 4.2%, the Weakest Month of 2026, and Electronics Supplies 62% of the Year's Gain

Profits at above-size industrial firms rose 4.2% in August after 11.2% in July. January to August profits reached ¥5.27 trillion, up 15.7%, with a 5.66% margin.


Electronics profits rose 110% and supplied 62% of the increase. Metals, coal and chemicals rode prices, utilities fell 12%, and foreign-invested firms grew just 2.3%. August 2025's 20.4% jump set a high base.

Key signal: The profit recovery is an AI-hardware and commodity story. Consumer-facing industry has not joined, and September PMIs land before Golden Week.
