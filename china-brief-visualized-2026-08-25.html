STORY 1 · AI & TECH
4.35GHz, Not 2nm: Xiaomi's Xring O3 Wins on Clock Speed, Concedes the Node

Xiaomi's Xring O3, unveiled Aug 24, is a 3nm, 10-core SoC with a 4.35GHz prime core (a mobile-chip first) and the first mobile use of LPDDR6 memory. It debuts in the Xiaomi 18 Fold and Pad 9 Pro Max in September at a modest 200K-300K unit target.


Qualcomm's and MediaTek's next-gen flagships are both moving to TSMC's 2nm node — a full generation ahead of the O3's 3nm. The clock-speed record is real, but it's being used to paper over a density gap Xiaomi's own roadmap concedes.

Key signal: Treat the O3 as a cost-exposure hedge after a memory-cost-driven profit miss, not yet a merchant-silicon substitution at scale.

STORY 2 · AI & TECH
$900M, -7% Same Day: XPeng's Robotics Unit Gets a Record Round the Stock Didn't Buy

XPeng Robotics (home of the Iron humanoid) raised $900M+ at a $6.3B post-money valuation on Aug 24 — the largest single private round yet in China's embodied-AI sector — led by IDG Capital with Tencent and Alibaba as strategic co-investors.


The same day, XPeng reported a Q2 earnings miss and XPEV shares fell ~7%. No unit economics, deployment volumes, or customer contracts accompanied the robotics valuation.

Key signal: A hot subsidiary valuation didn't offset investor concern about the parent's core EV results — funding-market enthusiasm and public-market judgment diverged on the same day.

STORY 3 · FINANCE & MACRO
¥800 Billion, Five Months Late: Beijing's Investment Lifeline Finally Opens for Bids

NDRC's ¥800B ($119B) New Policy-Backed Financing Instruments program, announced in spring 2026, has only now opened to project pitches from local governments. Caitong Securities estimates deployment is still "at least another month or so" away.


July data already shows the slowdown broadening (industrial output +4.5% y/y, retail sales +0.6%, FAI -6.7% Jan-Jul), while MOF's Liao Min says H2 fiscal-financial coordination policy is still being drafted, not launched.

Key signal: Track disbursement data, not announcement size — this tool's headline scale is arriving well after the growth window it was meant to support.
