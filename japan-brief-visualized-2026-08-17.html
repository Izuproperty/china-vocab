Story Analysis
STORY ONE · AI & TECH
Rakuten Posts First Net Profit in Six Years as AI-Powered Ads Cut Purchase Time 41%
Rakuten's April–June results ended a 22-quarter losing streak, with the advertising business — powered by AI search and targeted placements on Ichiba and Travel — growing faster than the group overall. Rakuten Mobile's losses are narrowing rather than widening for the first time since the carrier launched in 2020.
Q2 FY2026 YoY Growth by Metric
Investor Takeaway
Operating leverage from AI tooling shows up first in advertising, not in Mobile — the segment to watch for confirmation is whether Mobile churn keeps improving toward breakeven, since that has been the multi-year drag on group profitability.
SIGNAL
AI chat on Ichiba: 41% faster time-to-purchase, +17% average order value — disclosed, not just claimed.
WATCH
Mobile churn trend into Q3 — 1.3% is the best print yet, but one quarter doesn't confirm a breakeven path.
RISK
AI-search ad tooling becomes standard across Japanese e-commerce, eroding Rakuten's near-term differentiation.
STORY TWO · INDUSTRIAL & ROBOTICS
Kawasaki Heavy Raises Full-Year Profit Outlook as Robotics Segment Leads a Q1 Beat
Kawasaki's Q1 FY2026 business profit rose sharply on cost control and tariff refunds in its Precision Systems & Equipment (robotics) segment — a different driver than the Nvidia physical-AI platform launch and NEDO tactile-data project the company announced days later.
Consolidated Business Profit (¥bn)
Investor Takeaway
Treat the earnings beat and the physical-AI coalition-building as two separate tracks — the Q1 profit improvement is cost discipline and one-off tariff refunds, not yet AI-driven demand.
SIGNAL
Precision Systems & Equipment (robotics) segment was the primary driver of the Q1 beat.
WATCH
NEDO tactile-dataset collection (Aug 2026–Jul 2027) as the bridge from cost-driven profit to AI-driven revenue.
RISK
US tariff refunds are a one-off; guidance raise could prove hard to repeat without them.
STORY THREE · MACRO & BOJ
Japan's Cabinet Office Releases Preliminary Q2 GDP Today, With Consensus Pointing to a Third Straight Expansion
Today's preliminary Q2 GDP print is the last major data checkpoint before the BOJ's September 17–18 meeting. Consensus among 10 private-sector think tanks points to roughly +2.1% annualized growth, consumption-led — a number the market will read primarily for its composition, not just its headline size.
Quarterly Real GDP Growth, Annualized (%)
Investor Takeaway
A consumption-led beat supports the September-hike thesis already priced into JGB curves; an export- or inventory-driven print would leave more room for the BOJ's cautious camp to wait for October.
SIGNAL
Private consumption seen +0.5% q/q on real-wage gains and easing food inflation — the composition to watch.
WATCH
August 21 CPI print — the next hard checkpoint before the September 17–18 BOJ meeting.
RISK
A soft or export-driven print hands the BOJ's wait-for-more-data camp fresh grounds to push the hike to October.
