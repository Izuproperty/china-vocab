Why PFN is the node to watch
Preferred Networks sits outside the Noetra consortium and owns its MN-Core accelerator stack. The Sarvam pact gives it an export channel into a sovereign-AI market deliberately avoiding US hyperscaler lock-in.
Economic-security framing
The statement calls AI an "era-defining general-purpose technology" tied to economic security (keizai anzen hosho) — the same lens Japan applies to chips. Diplomacy is now an extension of industrial policy.
What would make it bankable
No compute or capital figures were disclosed. The test is whether Sarvam-PFN converts to MN-Core deployments or model licensing within a few quarters, backed by export finance from Tokyo.

USDJPY 2026: Interventions vs. the Trend
The ambush doctrine
Reuters reported July 1 that the MOF now favors unannounced strikes on speculative yen shorts over defending public lines. Silence is the policy tool; confirmation waits for the end-of-July monthly data.
Why intervention alone fails
A record ¥11.73T bought a month of relief before the trend reasserted. The rate differential against a BOJ normalizing gradually from 1% is the driver — intervention rents time, it does not set the level.
The real variable: July 31
Board members talk of a ~2% neutral rate; markets doubt the pace. A gradual BOJ likely means 160-162 gets retested. The alternative reading — a pure positioning squeeze with no official hand — is alive until the data prints.
