SPECIAL EDITION · GEOPOLITICS · APEC
Xi Pledges Tariff Cuts as He Hosts Trump and Putin in Shenzhen, While MOFCOM Threatens Countermeasures Over Washington's Chip-Rental Rule

Xi opened APEC in Shenzhen on Nov 18 promising tariff cuts and support for the WTO-centred multilateral trading system (多边贸易体制), as Trump and Putin (普京) arrived for the first US-China-Russia leaders' meeting on Nov 19.


MOFCOM (商务部) threatened unnamed countermeasures (反制措施) over the Nov 16 US chip-rental rule. China's trade with APEC partners rose 25.4% to ¥27.08 trillion ($4.04 trillion) in Jan-Oct.

Key signal: Beijing paired open-trade promises with a chip retaliation threat; the trilateral readouts and any named countermeasure will shape truce talks before the December G20.

STORY 1 · AI & TECH · AGENTS
Tencent's AI Build-Out Turns Q3 Free Cash Flow to Minus ¥69.1 Billion ($10.3 Billion) as WorkBuddy Passes 10 Million Daily Users

Tencent (腾讯) Q3 revenue rose 8% to ¥207.3 billion ($30.9 billion), but capex (资本开支) of ¥81.4 billion ($12.1 billion) pushed free cash flow (自由现金流) to minus ¥69.1 billion ($10.3 billion).


WorkBuddy passed 10 million daily users with a $500 million+ revenue run rate; ByteDance's Doubao (豆包) Work has 8 million and ¥2 billion ($300 million) enterprise ARR.

Key signal: Agent revenue is now measurable but small next to quarterly capex, so Tencent's valuation rests on WorkBuddy monetising before cash flow recovers.

STORY 2 · AI & TECH · CHIPS
Chip Designers Receive Foundry Price Notices of 10% to Over 20% as Hua Hong and CXMT Report Full Fabs and Tight DRAM

Designers report foundry price increase (涨价) notices of 10% to over 20% from Q1 2027, a 4-5 point gross margin (毛利率) hit if not passed on.


Hua Hong (华虹) expects 100% utilisation, SMIC (中芯国际) guided 2026 revenue above $12 billion, and CXMT (长鑫存储) sees tight DRAM through Q4.

Key signal: Price increases squeeze chip designers, while full mainland fabs get pricing power and have little spare capacity to take shifted orders.

STORY 3 · FINANCE · FISCAL STIMULUS
Renmin University Economist Proposes a ¥3 Trillion ($447 Billion) Bond-Funded Stimulus After October Data Show Demand Weakening

Renmin University's Wang Xiaosong proposed ¥3 trillion ($447 billion) of bond-funded fiscal stimulus (财政刺激), about 2% of GDP, led by ¥1.2 trillion ($179 billion) for household income.


October retail sales grew 0.4% and investment fell 8.1% year to date; the CEWC in December sets 2027 policy.

Key signal: The ¥3 trillion proposal marks the top of the stimulus range; a package near it would lift consumer stocks and push bond yields off record lows.
