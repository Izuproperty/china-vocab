STORY 1 · AI & TECH
ByteDance Is in Advanced Talks to Buy Custom AI Chips From MediaTek, Five Months After a Qualcomm Deal for Millions of Units

Bloomberg reported on October 15 that ByteDance is in advanced talks to buy custom data-center ASICs from MediaTek. No volume, price or date has been disclosed and neither company has confirmed it.


It would be ByteDance's third capped supply line after rationed Nvidia H200s and a Qualcomm ASIC deal in May, and MediaTek's second big ASIC client after Google.

Key signal: ByteDance is adding a third chip supply line under US performance caps, and the deal stays unconfirmed and exposed to thresholds Washington can lower and to Beijing's push for domestic chips.

STORY 2 · ROBOTICS
AgiBot Nears a ¥1–1.5 Billion Round at a ¥50 Billion Valuation, a Raise That Pushes Its Hong Kong IPO Past Year-End

AgiBot is near a ¥1–1.5 billion round at about ¥50 billion, roughly 48 times its ¥1.05 billion of 2025 revenue, per Caijing on October 13.


Hong Kong's 120-day rule pushes its IPO past year-end. Unitree, the listed peer, is down more than 60% from its peak but still trades near 100 times 2025 sales.

Key signal: AgiBot chose a private round at about 48 times 2025 sales over a quick listing, which delays its IPO past year-end and leaves the commercial-demand question for the prospectus to answer.

STORY 3 · FINANCE & MACRO
September New Loans Hit ¥1.08 Trillion Against a ¥975 Billion Forecast, While M2 Growth Fell to an 18-Month Low of 7.2%

September new loans rose to ¥1.08 trillion against a ¥975 billion forecast and ¥60 billion in August, helped by quarter-end and new policy-based financial instruments.


M2 slowed to 7.2%, an 18-month low, and M1 to 3.2%, so money held for spending shrank as lending rose.

Key signal: September's loan beat came from seasonal and policy-instrument lending while M1 and M2 both slowed, so the data do not show a turn in private credit demand.
