STORY 1 · AI & TECH · AUTOS / DEVICES
Xiaomi's EV and AI Unit Swings to a ¥3.4 Billion ($507 Million) Quarterly Loss as Deliveries Fall 7% and Memory Costs Squeeze Phone Margins

Xiaomi (小米) reported Q3 revenue of ¥107.24 billion ($16.0 billion), down 5.2%, and adjusted net profit of ¥4.88 billion ($728 million), down 56.8%. Car revenue fell 19.9% to ¥22.7 billion ($3.38 billion) on 100,975 deliveries (交付量), so revenue per car dropped about 14%.


Segment gross margin (毛利率) fell to 19.2% from 25.5% and phone margin to 9.2% from 11.1%, with memory prices cited. Lei Jun's (雷军) 550,000 target needs about 106,700 deliveries a month in November and December, roughly three times the Q3 pace.

Key signal: Cars and phones lost margin in the same quarter, which removes the idea that phones fund the EV and AI build-out; a segment margin back above 20% and falling inventory would mark Q3 as the low.

STORY 2 · AI & TECH · FRONTIER TECH / STANDARDS
MIIT Forms a Brain-Computer Interface Standards Working Group as Wuhan Opens BCI Standards Week, With 40+ Standards Targeted by 2028

MIIT (工信部) set up a new working group under its brain-computer interface (脑机接口) standards committee on Nov 23, ahead of BCI Standards Week in Wuhan on Nov 25-26. Nine industry standards are already approved for drafting; the targets are 40+ by 2028 and 80+ by 2030.


CAICT (中国信通院) counts 250+ Chinese BCI firms and forecasts a ¥6.14 billion ($915 million) market by 2028. The Beinao-1 (北脑一号) implant has reached 40+ patients and seeks device registration next year.

Key signal: Standards are arriving before revenue, as with humanoids; device registration and insurance billing will decide demand, and 250 firms chasing a ¥6 billion ($900 million) market points to consolidation.

STORY 3 · FINANCE · MONETARY POLICY / LIQUIDITY
PBOC Rolls Over Only ¥800 Billion ($119 Billion) of ¥1 Trillion ($149 Billion) in Maturing MLF, Its Second Straight Monthly Net Drain

The PBOC (中国人民银行) runs an ¥800 billion ($119 billion) one-year MLF (中期借贷便利) today against ¥1 trillion ($149 billion) maturing, a ¥200 billion ($29.8 billion) net withdrawal (净回笼), the fourth this year. A ¥500 billion ($74.5 billion) seven-day reverse repo (逆回购) runs alongside.


With the ¥300 billion ($44.7 billion) outright repo drain earlier in November, about ¥500 billion ($74.5 billion) of medium-term funds leave the system this month. October loans contracted and loan growth hit a record-low 4.7%.

Key signal: A strong yuan gives the PBOC room to ease, and it is draining instead while exchanges loosen margin rules; stable interbank rates into month-end would show the drain is removing idle cash, and a weak PMI would test the stance.
