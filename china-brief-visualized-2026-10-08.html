STORY 1 · AI & TECH
DeepSeek's Pre-IPO Round Grows to ¥80 Billion or More, With Tencent and CATL Writing the Largest Checks

Bloomberg reported on October 6 that DeepSeek is close to raising at least ¥80 billion, led by Tencent and CATL, after opening with a ¥50 billion target. The total could approach ¥100 billion at a valuation of about ¥500 billion.


The price sits near August's reported $74 billion pre-money, so the extra demand went into size. A larger private raise funds DeepSeek's Ascend data-center build and reduces what the STAR listing has to raise.

Key signal: DeepSeek's round grew in size while the price held, so its STAR Market listing will matter more as an exit for mainland holders than as a source of capital, and the open CAC probe is the main thing that could move its timing.

STORY 2 · AI & TECH
Moonshot Targets Up to $5 Billion in a Hong Kong IPO After Kimi's Revenue Run Rate Tripled to $1 Billion Since June

Moonshot closed its private round at about $50 billion and plans a Hong Kong IPO of up to $5 billion in Q1 2027, with Bank of America coordinating and CICC, Deutsche Bank and Goldman Sachs as sponsors.


ARR rose from $300 million in June to about $1 billion, with $2 billion targeted by December. That puts the valuation at about 50 times current run rate, below DeepSeek's reported multiple and above Kling's.

Key signal: Moonshot's IPO will be priced on whether a $1 billion run rate built in four months proves durable and profitable, and its terms will show how far Beijing's screening of US-origin capital reaches into a Hong Kong offering.

STORY 3 · FINANCE & MACRO
Reserves Fall $38 Billion in September While the PBOC Buys the Most Gold Since 2023 Into a 6.6% Price Drop

SAFE said FX reserves fell $38.1 billion to $3.4003 trillion in September, citing a stronger dollar and lower global asset prices after the Fed's first hike of the year.


The PBOC added 740,000 ounces of gold while the price fell 6.6%, so the market value of its gold dropped by $26.6 billion even as holdings rose.

Key signal: September's $38 billion reserve decline came from bond and currency valuation after the Fed's hike, while the PBOC sped up gold buying into a falling price, so this month's settlement and FX-purchase data are where any real outflow would show.
