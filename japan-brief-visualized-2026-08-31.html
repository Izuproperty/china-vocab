AI & Tech · Story 1
Rakuten Securities Bets on Generative AI for Customer Support

Rakuten Securities will launch an agent-powered generative-AI chat layer on September 2, capable of interpreting colloquial questions rather than fixed menu prompts — and has flagged interest in eventually extending into investment-advisory consultations, a regulated activity under FSA rules.

The move follows Rakuten Group's first quarterly net profit in six years, aided by AI-powered commerce tools that already cut Ichiba's time-to-purchase 41% and lifted average order value 17%. Extending that toolkit into brokerage is a lower-risk trial before any move toward AI-driven advice.

Rakuten Ichiba AI Chat: Measured Impact (Q2 FY2026)
SIGNAL
Financial-services AI chat is moving from marketing gimmick to measurable conversion tool at group scale.
WATCH
Whether Rakuten Securities pushes into "advisory consultations" and how the FSA treats that boundary.
RISK
No compliance framework for AI-driven advisory has been disclosed — regulatory friction is the likely gating factor.
Investor takeaway: Brokerage AI chat itself is low-stakes; the real optionality is whether Japan's FSA lets AI agents cross into advisory territory, which would reshape retail brokerage cost structures.
Semiconductors · Story 2
A Seasonings Company's Grip on a Critical Chip Material Turns Into Geopolitical Leverage

Ajinomoto reportedly cut ABF chip-packaging film supply to China by 30%, prioritizing Japanese and overseas core accounts. Ajinomoto controls over 95% of the global ABF market; China's self-sufficiency sits below 5%.

The move follows China's rare-earth export curbs on Japan-linked end users — a reversal of leverage in a material where Japan, not China, holds the chokepoint. Every account attributes the cut to AI-driven capacity allocation rather than policy.

Goldman Sachs: Global ABF Supply-Demand Gap Forecast
SIGNAL
Japan holds an under-appreciated chokepoint (ABF film) in the same AI supply chain where China holds rare earths.
WATCH
Whether China's three domestic ABF alternatives (Huazheng, Newface, Hongchang) clear multi-year reliability qualification.
RISK
The 30% volume-cut figure is unconfirmed, sourced only to supply-chain contacts; only the ~30% price hike is confirmed.
Investor takeaway: Every FC-BGA package under an Nvidia, AMD, or Intel accelerator runs through Ajinomoto's film — a widening structural supply gap through 2028 is a persistent input-cost tailwind for Ajinomoto and a cost headwind for chip packagers.
Macro & BOJ · Story 3
Japan's July Data Delivers a Split Verdict

Unemployment fell to a post-pandemic low of 2.3%, well below the 2.5% consensus. But industrial production (-1.6% m/m) and retail sales (-1.6%) both missed sharply, dragged by weak auto and chip-equipment output.

The split lands alongside MoF's disclosure of a record ¥15.39tn FX intervention over July-August — the largest monthly total ever, pushing 2026's cumulative total above ¥27tn, already past 2024's full-year record.

July 2026: Actual vs. Consensus
SIGNAL
Tight labor market keeps the September hike case alive — Himino's Aug 27 speech left that door open.
WATCH
Whether MoF steps in with a third FX intervention before Sept 17-18, or lets a hawkish hike do the currency-support work.
RISK
Weak output and spending give the Board's wait-for-more-data camp fresh ammunition against a September move.
Investor takeaway: September's decision has to reconcile a labor market that says "hike" with an output/spending picture that says "wait" — positioning around the meeting should account for both a hike and a hold scenario.
