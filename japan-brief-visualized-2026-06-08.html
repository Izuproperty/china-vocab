Structural Problem
The BOJ-Fed rate gap is ~300bp. At that spread, carry trade demand for dollar over yen is structural. Japan spent ¥11.73T to defend 160 in April-May; the rate gap alone reversed those gains. Intervention is a delay tactic, not a solution.
Blackout Vulnerability
During June 9-15, no BOJ communication is available to anchor expectations. This is the window where yen drift is fastest — markets test 160+ without central bank verbal guidance as a friction. MOF can still intervene but with diminishing credibility.
June 16 Variable
The 25bp hike itself is priced at ~80%. What matters is Ueda's press conference language on September: if he weakens conditionality, September hike probability could move from 10-15% toward 35-40%, providing the durable yen anchor that interventions cannot.
