STORY 1 · AI & TECH · FOUNDATION MODELS
Four Zhipu Founders Worked on a 2018 to 2021 Research Project Co-Funded by an Institute MI5 Says Has "Very Strong Links" to China's Spy Agency

Bloomberg matched Li Juanzi, Tang Jie, Xu Bin and CEO Zhang Peng to a social-media event-detection and relationship-mapping project co-funded by CGTRI and the NSFC. MI5's September 30 alert said CGTRI funds research for the Ministry of State Security and warned that continuing such work could bring prosecution.


Zhipu is already on the US Entity List and was named in the September distillation advisory. The new link runs through a UK security service, which gives British and European buyers of GLM weights and APIs a specific compliance trigger.

Key signal: Zhipu's overseas developer push is the exposed part of the business, because procurement and compliance teams abroad now have a named intelligence link to cite; domestic government and SOE revenue is less affected.

STORY 2 · AI & TECH · AUTONOMOUS DRIVING
Robovan Maker Neolix Raises Nearly $400 Million Led by Boyu Capital and the National AI Fund, Claiming 30,000 Vehicles in 300 Cities

Neolix announced the Series E on October 20 and called it the largest autonomous-driving financing in China this year. Alibaba's AEF NextGen fund, Granite Asia, Vertex Growth and GAC Capital joined, and earlier backers returned. No valuation was disclosed.


The company claims more than 30,000 driverless vans in over 300 cities across 20 countries. The only named overseas approval is a June entry into Malaysia's regulatory sandbox in Cyberjaya. Proceeds go to service hubs abroad and end-to-end model training.

Key signal: A state AI fund co-leading the round marks driverless logistics as an export industry, but each foreign market needs its own road permits and Neolix has published no revenue or unit economics.

STORY 3 · FINANCE & MACRO · EARNINGS
CATL's Q3 Revenue Jumps 58.6% but Profit Rises Only 14.5% as a New Battery Consumption Tax and Lower-Value Orders Squeeze Margins; Shares Fall 9% to 11%

Revenue reached ¥165.22B and net profit ¥21.25B, down 5.8% from Q2. Gross margin fell to 20.69% from 23.15%, and nine-month operating cash flow dropped 23.6% as receivables and inventories grew about 50%.


Management blamed a shift to lower-value products. A 2% battery consumption tax started in September, so Q4 carries three months of it, and the export VAT rebate ends in 2027. Morgan Stanley flagged energy-storage price competition.

Key signal: The Ministry of Finance is taxing a sector it once subsidised while the anti-involution campaign blocks price cuts, so the leading supplier absorbed the cost in margin, and smaller battery makers have less room still.
