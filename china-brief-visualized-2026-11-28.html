STORY 1 · AI & TECH · HUMANOID ROBOTICS
AgiBot's G2 Moves From Inspection to Speaker Assembly on Longcheer's Line, but Discloses No Cycle Time or Unit Count

AgiBot (智元机器人) said on Nov 27 its Jingling G2 now installs speaker BOX modules on the line of Longcheer (龙旗科技) and runs in parallel production (并线生产) beside human stations. The robots already ran the full tablet quality-inspection (质检) section at Longcheer's Nanchang plant from May. The G2 line solution also won UNIDO's Deployable Solution Award the same day.


In a June 23-28 live run, eight G2 units performed 64,828 operations on 17,625 tablets at 99.99% success (company figures). AgiBot passed 15,000 units in June and claims 100,000+ a year of flexible capacity. It is valued near ¥50 billion ($7.5 billion) on 2025 revenue of ¥1.05 billion ($158 million).

Key signal: A second, harder task at the same paying customer points to real demand, but Longcheer is a friendly buyer with a reported framework order for close to 1,000 robots, and throughput against human workers is still undisclosed.

STORY 2 · AI & TECH · EMBODIED AI DATA
PaXini Opens a 700-Person Robot Data Factory in Wuhan as Embodied AI's Bottleneck Shifts to Training Data

PaXini (帕西尼) opened a super data factory (数据工厂) in Wuhan's Dongxihu District (东西湖区) on Nov 27, with 220+ collection units (采集单元) and about 700 staff recording touch, vision, pose and action data on robot-agnostic glove rigs. It took about eight months from the March groundbreaking. Wuhan is one of five sites; a ~30-member regional consortium including China Telecom and Sugon (中科曙光) launched alongside.


Robots lack an internet-scale data set, so makers pay people to perform tasks in front of sensors. EngineAI cited 50,000 hours of pretraining data on Wednesday. CEO Xu Jincheng (许晋诚) disclosed no hours collected, customers or prices.

Key signal: Data collection earns from whichever robot maker wins, but 700 staff make it a labour-intensive service whose margins depend on undisclosed per-hour pricing, and glove-rig data may transfer poorly across robot bodies.

STORY 3 · FINANCE · INDUSTRIAL DATA
October Industrial Profits Jump 20.1%, Fastest in Five Months, on a Weak Base and AI Hardware

NBS (国家统计局) said on Nov 27 that profits at above-scale industrial firms (规模以上工业企业) rose 20.1% in October (September 14.4%). Jan-Oct profits rose 16% to ¥6.737 trillion ($1.01 trillion); manufacturing ¥5.113 trillion ($769 billion), +17.5%. Electronics profits doubled and supplied 9.7 of the 16 points. High-tech manufacturing (高技术制造业) rose 50.9%, optical fibre (光纤) 670%, furniture -52.4%.


October 2025 profits fell 5.5%, so a base effect (基数效应) inflates the print, and 5.7% producer-price inflation lifted mining and refining. October retail sales grew only 0.4%. November PMI is due Monday, November 30.

Key signal: The data supports earnings at data-centre hardware suppliers and lowers the odds of broad stimulus, but profit growth now rests on AI capex and commodity prices, both of which can turn.
