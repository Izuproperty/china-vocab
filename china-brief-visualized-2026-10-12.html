STORY 1 · AI & TECH
JAKA Debuts a Heavy-Duty Wheeled Humanoid at Shanghai's Industry Fair, Ten Months After Its STAR Market IPO Was Terminated

JAKA launched the Kargo50 heavy-duty wheeled humanoid and the Zu50 cobot at CIIF, which opens today in Shanghai. Its smaller Kargo10 cut tote-handling time from 105 to 28 seconds, by the company's account.


JAKA leads China's cobot market but its STAR IPO was terminated in December 2025 after questions over revenue recognition. The Kargo50 has to beat cheaper forklift robots and AMRs on cost per move.

Key signal: JAKA is repositioning as an embodied-AI company after a failed IPO, and the Kargo50 will only move its valuation once a named customer buys it at a disclosed price.

STORY 2 · AI & TECH
CXMT Targets End-2026 Launch of 4F² DDR5 Server Memory, a Cell Design Samsung Plans to Adopt in 2028

CXMT plans a DDR5 RDIMM server module on a 4F² cell with hybrid bonding by end-2026. The design cuts cell area by a third using a vertical surrounded-gate transistor.


Samsung's stated 4F² plan is for 2028. CXMT has disclosed no yield, capacity or customer, and RDIMM qualification pushes any revenue into 2027.

Key signal: An on-schedule 4F² server module would put CXMT on a new DRAM architecture before Samsung without EUV tools, but it has published no yield, capacity or customer data to back the claim.

STORY 3 · FINANCE & MACRO
Week Ahead: Exports Seen Near 30%, PPI Near 4.5% on Oil, and Banks Need a Lending Rebound After August's ¥60 Billion

September trade and CPI/PPI land on Wednesday, Oct 14. Forecasts put exports at 25-30% growth and PPI at about 4.5% as Brent averaged ~$100 in September.


PBOC credit data also arrive this week after August new loans of just ¥60 billion. The consensus for September is about ¥975 billion.

Key signal: This week's trade, price and credit releases will show whether growth still rests on exports and oil-driven factory prices while household borrowing contracts, and a weak loan print would strengthen the case for PBOC easing.
