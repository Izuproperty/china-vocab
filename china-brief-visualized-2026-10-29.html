STORY 1 · AI & TECH · CHIPS
Moore Threads Posts Its First Quarterly Profit as Q3 Revenue Rises Nearly 19-Fold to ¥1.55 Billion, and Plans a Hong Kong Secondary Listing

Moore Threads reported Q3 revenue of ¥1.55 billion (about $230 million) on October 28, with net profit of ¥49.26 million (about $7.3 million) and ¥20.07 million (about $3.0 million) excluding one-offs, its first profitable quarter and ahead of a 2027 target. Nine-month revenue was ¥3.29 billion (about $490 million), up 319%. It plans a Hong Kong secondary listing.


Domestic AI labs and government buyers drove MTT S5000 orders, and a 100,000-GPU cluster with JD.com is under construction. Quarterly R&D of ¥570 million (about $85 million) took more than a third of revenue, and the nine-month gross margin is about 54%.

Key signal: State and domestic-lab procurement now produces profits at a second listed accelerator maker after Cambricon, but the margin is thin and Entity List limits on foundry capacity cap how fast revenue can grow, so Q4 revenue holding near ¥1.5 billion is the test for the valuation.

STORY 2 · AI & TECH · ROBOTICS
ByteDance's Seed Team Posts Dozens of Robot Jobs, From Dexterous Hands to Gear Reducers, Signalling It Will Build Its Own Robot Bodies

ByteDance's Seed team posted dozens of robotics roles on October 27 covering robot bodies, dexterous hands, gear reducers, wiring and embedded drivers, plus software for instruction-to-execution control and a data loop from collection to evaluation. A senior manipulation post listed ¥95,000 to ¥120,000 a month (about $14,000 to $18,000).


ByteDance has released GR-1 through GR-3 robot models since 2023, built the ByteMini dual-arm robot, shipped 1,000+ logistics robots, and joined X Square Robot's ¥1 billion (about $150 million) round in January. Analysts describe Seed building hardware for data while Volcano Engine sells embodied models externally.

Key signal: A cash-rich model lab is moving into robot hardware to generate its own manipulation data, which brings prototype orders for reducer and hand suppliers and new competition for startups selling bodies plus models, though nothing beyond job posts has been announced.

STORY 3 · FINANCE & MACRO · LIQUIDITY
PBOC Adds a Record ¥1.01 Trillion in a Day and the Five-Year Yield Closes at an All-Time Low as Beijing Lines Up ¥2.2 Trillion of Bond Sales for November and December

Banks drew the full ¥1 trillion (about $149 billion) overnight reverse repo window on October 27 at 1.25%, plus ¥412 billion (about $61 billion) of seven-day repos, for a record ¥1.01 trillion (about $150 billion) net injection. The five-year yield closed at 1.340% on October 28, the lowest since 2002.


Governments are likely to raise at least ¥2.2 trillion (about $330 billion) net in November and December, taking 2026 to at least ¥13.35 trillion (about $2.0 trillion). Policy rates are unchanged, so the PBOC is supplying volume to let banks absorb the supply.

Key signal: The central bank is funding the banks that buy the year-end bond wave, which keeps yields falling while debt rises; record-low yields squeeze bank margins and widen the gap with a hiking Fed, a headwind for the yuan.
