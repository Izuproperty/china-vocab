SPECIAL EDITION · GEOPOLITICS · SHENZHEN SUMMIT
Xi and Trump Agree to Draft an Action Plan for "Strategic Stability," While the Trilateral With Putin Produces a Peace Declaration and Nothing on Tariffs or Chips

Xi and Trump agreed on Nov 19 to draw up an action plan (行动计划) for a constructive strategic stability relationship (建设性战略稳定关系). China's readout says Trump stated the US does not support Taiwan independence (台独); Washington had not confirmed it.


The 90-minute trilateral with Putin (普京) yielded a four-point Xinhua declaration on UN reform, refined-fuel (成品油) supply, ICC jurisdiction and nuclear war (核战争). It contains nothing on tariffs or chips.

Key signal: The summit created a process with no deadline, while the trade truce and the chip-licence window both run out in the next three months.

STORY 1 · AI & TECH · CHIPS
Nvidia Ends Licensed H200 Shipments to China After a $2.4 Billion Quarter, as Washington's Remote-Access Chip Rule Takes Effect

Nvidia (英伟达) interim CEO Colette Kress said licensed Hopper shipments to China will stop. They were $2.4 billion last quarter, about 22% of $11.05 billion from China-headquartered customers.


The remote-access (远程访问) rule took effect on Nov 19. Data centres serving Chinese firms need an export licence (出口许可证) by Feb 17, 2027.

Key signal: Both licensed sales and rented offshore capacity now face closure, pushing Chinese labs toward Huawei and Cambricon (寒武纪) parts that still trail Nvidia.

STORY 2 · AI & TECH · FOUNDATION MODELS
SenseTime Open-Sources Loop-9B, a 9-Billion-Parameter Model That Runs Its Middle Layers Twice to Do 56 Layers of Work

SenseTime (商汤科技) released Loop-9B under Apache 2.0 on Nov 17. Layer reuse (层复用) gives 56 layers of compute from 32 stored layers, with a 512K-token context window (上下文窗口).


SenseTime claims parity with Qwen3.8-27B on selected agent tests but published no scores. A third-party test measured 2.2-3.0 tokens per second unquantised.

Key signal: Looping trades memory for compute, which fits China's HBM and accelerator shortage, but the second pass slows every token served.

STORY 3 · FINANCE · CURRENCY
PBOC Sets the Yuan's Strongest Fixing Since July 2022 at 6.7118, but Still 464 Pips Weaker Than the Market Expected

The PBOC (中国人民银行) set the central parity fixing (中间价) at 6.7118 on Nov 18, the strongest since July 2022, while spot traded near 6.669 on Nov 19.


Exporters' FX settlement (结汇) supports the yuan, up more than 4% this year; the PBOC raised the FX risk reserve ratio (外汇风险准备金率) in early November to slow gains.

Key signal: The PBOC is letting the yuan rise while capping the pace; a narrower fix-to-estimate gap would show it accepting faster gains.
