STORY 1 · AI & TECH
NTT's IOWN Network Becomes the Backbone of an AI-Enabled Defense Command System

Japan's Ministry of Defense will adopt NTT's IOWN All-Photonic Network (APN) as the core backbone connecting Self-Defense Forces units and bases nationwide — carrying radar, sensor, and drone data to command centers for AI-assisted analysis. The adoption will be written into the next National Defense Program Guidelines, with costs folded into the FY2027 budget and full operation targeted from FY2028.

IOWN / APN — Defense Rollout Timeline
Investor Takeaway
A government reference deployment at this scale gives NTT a stronger sales pitch for IOWN/APN to other latency-sensitive customers — but it also ties NTT's civilian infrastructure growth more tightly to the durability of Japan's defense-spending cycle.
SIGNAL
NTT shares rose on the report — market read as a positive commercialization data point for IOWN.
WATCH
Whether National Defense Program Guidelines language, due later this year, confirms scope and funding detail.
RISK
Defense budget items remain provisional pending December security-document revisions — funding could shift.
STORY 2 · INDUSTRIAL & AUTO
Honda and Nissan Standardize Vehicle Software — a Narrower Bet After Their Merger Collapsed

Honda and Nissan concluded a joint development agreement to standardize core ECUs, the in-vehicle OS, middleware, and control software for next-generation software-defined vehicles, targeting application from fiscal 2029 — a software-only successor to the 2024–25 merger talks that collapsed.

SDV Collaboration Scope — What's Shared vs. Separate
Investor Takeaway
This is cost-sharing on the expensive, commoditizing layer of SDV development — not a revival of the broader merger. The real test is whether shared specifications actually compress development timelines by FY2029.
SIGNAL
Both firms frame this as faster, cheaper R&D versus going it alone against Chinese and US SDV leaders.
WATCH
Concrete ECU/OS specification milestones between now and FY2029 as evidence the alliance is delivering.
RISK
Software-only alliances have historically delivered less integration benefit than full mergers.
STORY 3 · MACRO & POLICY
Defense Ministry Requests a Record ¥8.9 Trillion Budget, With AI and Drones at the Center

The Defense Ministry's FY2027 budget request rests on three pillars — AI/drones/missiles for "new ways of warfare," sustained operational capability, and a stronger domestic defense industrial base — with roughly 160 line items left unfunded pending December security-document revisions.

Defense Budget Request — Requested vs. Potential Final (¥ trillion)
Investor Takeaway
The headline ¥8.9tn understates likely final spending. Watch defense-industrial names (Mitsubishi Heavy, Kawasaki Heavy, IHI) and C4ISR/sensor suppliers (NEC, Fujitsu) as the security-document revisions clarify the ~160 blank line items.
SIGNAL
Request explicitly names AI, drones, and industrial-base strengthening as priority pillars.
WATCH
PM Takaichi's year-end revision of Japan's three core national-security documents.
RISK
MOF must balance this against a widening gap between whole-of-government spending requests and tax revenue.
