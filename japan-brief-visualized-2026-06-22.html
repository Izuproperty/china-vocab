Japan AI Policy Milestones Timeline
Japan AI governance has moved from no formal law (2024) to an active revision cycle (2026) in approximately 18 months — faster than most G7 peers outside the EU.
Governance Speed
6-month revision cycle is well ahead of Japan's typical annual budget-aligned policy cadence. Signals AI Policy Office has authority to accelerate outside the fiscal calendar.
Cybersecurity Angle
Explicit mention of frontier AI models enabling sophisticated cyberattacks opens a domestic AI security software investment thesis — market currently small relative to US/EU but growing.
EU Convergence
International cooperation and misinformation detection provisions mirror EU AI Act priorities. If frameworks converge, regulatory compliance costs for multinationals in both markets may decline.

Rapidus Watch
Design review volume from new customers by Sept-Oct 2026 is the key metric for assessing whether 2027 mass production timeline remains credible. Tenstorrent is the only publicly announced customer so far.
