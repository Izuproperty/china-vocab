STORY 1 · AI & TECH · FOUNDATION MODELS
Zhipu Puts GLM-5.4, Its First Loop-Architecture Flagship, on Its Coding Plan and Holds Back the API and Open Weights for a Safety Review

Zhipu (智谱) released GLM-5.4 on Nov 23 to Coding Plan subscribers ($18, $80 and $168 a month) with a 50% larger weekly quota. It runs weight-shared layers in a loop (循环架构) and Zhipu claims "2-3T-level" capability; 快科技 reported about 700 billion parameters.


API access waits on a safety evaluation (安全评估), and open weights (权重) have no date. One private coding test placed it at 33.2, against 33.6 for GPT-6.2 Astra and 34.7 for Opus 5.5, while finding it reasons on over 99% of steps.

Key signal: Zhipu is selling to subscribers before releasing weights, as Alibaba did with Qwen4-Max, which helps revenue and slows open-weight adoption; independent benchmarks and per-task cost will decide whether the loop design pays off.

STORY 2 · AI & TECH · PHYSICAL AI / ROBOTICS
Four Ministries Tell Care Homes to Pilot Service Robots and Exoskeletons, and Humanoid Stocks Lead a 3.1% ChiNext Rally

The NDRC (发改委), Ministry of Civil Affairs (民政部), NHC and Ministry of Education issued the State Council-approved 一老一小 plan on Nov 23. It encourages AI agents, service robots, wearables and exoskeletons (外骨骼) in care settings and sets 2030 targets of 73% nursing beds and 70% community facility coverage.


The plan has no budget. Demand rests on a caregiver shortage of over 5 million and a 60-plus population heading toward 400 million. Humanoid stocks led Monday's ChiNext gain of 3.08%, on the same day as the margin rule change.

Key signal: Elderly-care robots (养老机器人) now sit in national policy without funding; exoskeletons and care devices are closer to revenue than humanoids, so provincial tenders and silver-economy (银发经济) subsidies are the evidence to wait for.

STORY 3 · FINANCE · MARKETS / CSRC POLICY
Exchanges Cut the Margin Deposit Floor to 80% From 100%, Reversing January's Tightening, With Margin Debt at ¥2.2 Trillion ($330 Billion), Down From ¥3 Trillion ($450 Billion) in June

With CSRC (证监会) approval, the three exchanges cut the margin deposit ratio (融资保证金比例) to 80% from Nov 30, reversing January's rise to 100%. Margin loans (融资融券) are about ¥2.2 trillion ($330 billion), down more than 20% from a ¥3 trillion ($450 billion) late-June peak.


State media estimate up to ¥400 billion ($60.0 billion) of extra borrowing capacity. Shenzhen also drafted ChiNext market-maker (做市商) rules, and fee cuts total about ¥2.76 billion ($414 million).

Key signal: Margin debt fell because investors cut risk, so extra capacity may go unused; a sustained rise in the daily balance after Nov 30 would show retail leverage (杠杆) returning, and a flat one leaves support to the PBOC and the national team.
