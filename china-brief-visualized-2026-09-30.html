STORY 1 · AI & TECH
Spouses and Children Now Need Approval Too: Beijing Extends AI Travel Curbs to Executives' Families

Families of selected AI and chip executives at private firms now need approval to travel abroad, Bloomberg reported on September 28. Senior staff at companies including Alibaba and DeepSeek have needed it since May.


A State Council exit regulation effective September 15 allows outright bans in export-control and technology-trade cases, and Manus's co-founders were barred from leaving during Meta's failed $2 billion deal. The family rule gives officials leverage over executives with no case opened.

Key signal: Beijing now controls who travels at leading AI labs as well as who invests and what code leaves. Key-person risk in Chinese AI now includes whether founders can travel for roadshows and partner meetings.

STORY 2 · AI & TECH
30-Second Clips and an $18 Billion Mark: Kling 4.0 Arrives While Kuaishou Trades Below Its Own Video Unit

Kling 4.0 Flash opened to paying members on September 28, with the full model due in October. Clips run to 30 seconds, extendable to two minutes, with 10 keyframes, 15 reference inputs and 4K HDR output.


Kling's annualized revenue passed $500 million in March, but Q2 growth slowed to about 200% from about 300%. The July round valued the unit near $18 billion, while Kuaishou, down more than 60% in a year, is worth about $17 billion.

Key signal: Kuaishou's share price gives its core short-video business almost no value next to Kling's private mark. Kling's Q3 revenue in November decides which side moves.

STORY 3 · FINANCE & MACRO
PSL Cut to 1.5%, ¥700 Billion More in Relending, and the First Central Subsidy on Mortgage Interest

The PBOC cut the one-year PSL rate 25bp to 1.5% on September 29 and opened PSL to six infrastructure networks including computing power. Technology relending rose to ¥1.4 trillion and small-business and farm relending to ¥4.85 trillion.


From October 1 the central budget pays 1 point a year for up to five years on first-home mortgages up to ¥1 million, for homes up to 120 sqm and ¥1.5 million. The 7-day rate and LPR did not move.

Key signal: Easing is flowing to policy banks and targeted loans, and the mortgage subsidy aims at lower-tier starter homes. October mortgage issuance will show whether six months of household deleveraging ends.
