STORY 1 · AI & TECH
SoftBank Prices a Record ¥1 Trillion Retail Bond
SoftBank priced a seven-year, roughly ¥1 trillion retail bond today — the largest retail bond sale ever by a Japanese company, and its third record-setting retail issuance in under a year. The coupon landed near the top of the guided 4.3–4.9% range. Proceeds fund the ABB robotics acquisition and repay the bridge loan behind SoftBank's OpenAI stake.
SoftBank Retail Bond Issuance, 2025–2026
Investor Takeaway
Watch whether the Sept 7–16 retail offering period clears at the guided coupon or needs a sweetener — the real test of household appetite for SoftBank's AI-linked debt load.
SIGNAL
Third record retail bond in under a year — retail debt, not equity sales, is SoftBank's preferred AI-funding channel in 2026.
WATCH
Final coupon print and offering take-up rate Sept 7–16 — a live read on retail yield demands.
RISK
S&P rates SoftBank BB+, one notch below investment grade — credit-strain risk if AI capex returns disappoint.
STORY 2 · SEMICONDUCTORS
METI's Record ¥7.7tn Budget Request, ¥150bn More for Rapidus
METI filed a record ¥7.7268tn FY2027 budget request on Sept 1 — roughly 2.5x last year's ask — with ~¥1.4tn for AI, semiconductor, and robotics initiatives, including a ¥150bn capital injection for Rapidus first flagged Aug 21.
METI's ¥7.73 Trillion FY2027 Budget Request — Breakdown
Investor Takeaway
Budget requests are a floor, not a ceiling — the Finance Ministry trims before year-end Cabinet approval. Track how much of the ¥1.4tn AI/chip envelope survives intact.
SIGNAL
METI's request has grown roughly 2.5x year-on-year — the clearest budget-cycle evidence of Takaichi's ¥370tn blueprint translating into ministry spending.
WATCH
Cabinet approval of the final FY2027 budget, expected by year-end — the number that actually matters for Rapidus's funding runway.
RISK
Rapidus keeps drawing repeat capital injections rather than reaching self-sustaining project finance ahead of its 2027 mass-production deadline.
STORY 3 · MACRO & FISCAL
30-Year JGB Auction Clears With Firmer-Than-Average Demand
Thursday's 30-year JGB auction drew a 3.79x bid-to-cover ratio, above the 12-month average of 3.52x, even as the accepted yield rose to 4.080% — an orderly result that pushes back, for one auction cycle, against the more acute debt-selloff narrative.
30-Year JGB Auction: Yield vs. Bid-to-Cover, Last Two Sales
Investor Takeaway
Treat this auction as evidence demand exists at current yields, not confirmation it will at higher ones — the next 30- and 40-year sales, after the BOJ decision and FY2027 budget approval, are the more informative test.
SIGNAL
Bid-to-cover above the 12-month average despite yields at their highest since the auction series began climbing.
WATCH
The Sept 17–18 BOJ decision and year-end Cabinet approval of FY2027 spending — both land before the next long-dated auctions.
RISK
Government-wide FY2027 budget requests approach ¥130tn, a fourth straight record — rising supply the market must keep absorbing.
