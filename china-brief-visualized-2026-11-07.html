STORY 1 · AI & TECH · CHIPS / MATURE-NODE FOUNDRY
Hua Hong's Q3 Profit Rises 147% on Record $987 Million Revenue, as Price Increases on AI-Adjacent MCU and Flash Chips Lift Margins at China's Second Foundry

Hua Hong Semiconductor (华虹半导体) reported Q3 revenue of $987.1 million on November 5, up 19.9% y/y, with net profit up 147.1% to $123.4 million and gross margin at 24.6%. Excluding the newly consolidated Huali Microelectronics (华力微电子), revenue and margin both beat guidance. Q4 guidance is $1.08 to $1.09 billion at a 25% to 26% margin.


The biggest price increases came in MCUs (微控制器), then flash and power management. Peng Bai (白鹏) said more than half of products now go into AI-related applications and expects ASPs to rise 10% to 15% in 2027. North America revenue grew 75.1% and Europe roughly doubled, from small bases.

Key signal: AI server demand is raising prices for the mature-node chips around the accelerators, so Hua Hong's margin path depends on those prices holding as Wuxi's Fab 9 adds capacity and Huali's roughly 40% margin flows through.

STORY 2 · AI & TECH · ROBOTICS / EMBODIED AI
A CNPC Unit Takes 60% of an Oilfield Robotics Venture With Unitree, Giving the Robot Maker a State Energy Customer for Its Explosion-Proof Quadrupeds

Unitree (宇树科技) will form a venture with CNPC's Kunlun Manufacturing (昆仑制造) and AI developer Beijing Fanjie for inspection (巡检) and emergency response at oilfields, refineries, depots and gas stations, per a Beijing market regulator filing reported November 6. The CNPC side holds 60%; Unitree and Fanjie hold 20% each.


The product is the B2-Ex explosion-proof (防爆) quadruped. Kunlun Capital already took about 2.2% of Unitree's ¥6.1 billion ($910 million) IPO. Unitree's Q3 revenue rose 69.9% to ¥665 million ($100 million) while profit fell 17.4%, and only about 9% of humanoid revenue has come from industry.

Key signal: CNPC's majority stake gives Unitree a state industrial channel for its quadrupeds, but most service margin stays with the state side and the tie adds to Unitree's exposure to US restrictions.

STORY 3 · FINANCE & MACRO · RATES / BOND MARKET
China's 10-Year Yield Touches a Record 1.58% as the PBOC's New 1.25% Overnight Rate and a Tech Sell-Off Push Money Into Government Bonds

The 10-year government bond (十年期国债) yield hit 1.58% intraday on November 6, a record low per Bloomberg, down from about 1.64% before the PBOC's October 30 announcement. Equities fell the same day: the Shanghai Composite lost 1.10% and the CSI 300 1.44%, with Zhongji Innolight (中际旭创) down 9.19%.


Since November 2 the PBOC has run daily overnight reverse repos (隔夜逆回购) at 1.25%, below the 1.40% seven-day rate (七天逆回购) it is retiring as the policy rate, and injected ¥541 billion ($81 billion) on the first day. Huafa's credit scare has added safe-haven demand.

Key signal: The 1.25% overnight rate has pulled long yields to a record without an LPR cut, which cheapens year-end government issuance but squeezes bank margins until loan pricing follows.
