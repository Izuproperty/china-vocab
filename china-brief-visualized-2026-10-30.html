STORY 1 · AI & TECH · CHIPS
CXMT Will Start HBM3E Production With Yields of 20% to 30%, Against About 70% at SK Hynix, as Domestic AI Chipmakers Wait for Memory

Reuters reported on October 28 that CXMT will begin limited HBM3E production over the next two months with only 20% to 30% of chips usable, against about 70% at SK Hynix. Samples in a standard and a yield-improved version have gone to several domestic AI chip designers, and one source said performance sits closer to HBM3.


US controls since December 2024 block advanced HBM sales to China, and Huawei, Cambricon, MetaX and Iluvatar CoreX raised AI chip prices by 20% to 50% last month. CXMT's first-half DRAM profit of ¥77.6 billion (about $11.6 billion) can absorb the scrap cost of a low-yield line.

Key signal: Domestic HBM supply starts in volume terms but at about three times the silicon cost per good stack and one generation behind on performance, so accelerator prices stay high and Ascend-class output stays memory-limited until a customer qualifies the part and yields pass 50%.

STORY 2 · AI & TECH · ROBOTICS
Unitree's First Report as a Listed Company: Q3 Revenue Rises 70% to ¥665 Million, but Profit Falls 17% to Half the Consensus as R&D Triples and Inventory Reaches ¥1 Billion

Unitree reported Q3 revenue of ¥665 million (about $100 million), up 69.9%, and net profit of ¥112.7 million (about $17 million), down 17.4% and about half the ¥224 million (about $34 million) forecast. R&D rose 249.6% to ¥127 million (about $19 million) and selling expenses reached ¥230 million (about $34 million).


Inventory climbed to ¥1.02 billion (about $153 million) from ¥367 million (about $55 million) at end-2025, with no explanation. The market value of about ¥281.5 billion (about $42 billion) is roughly 106 times annualized Q3 sales.

Key signal: Spending and inventory are rising faster than 70% revenue growth, so the valuation now depends on Q4 sell-through and on evidence that industrial customers, not state and university buyers, are taking the extra units.

STORY 3 · POLICY & MACRO · PLENUM
The Fifth Plenum Closes, Confirming Discipline Cases Against 34 Central Committee Members Including Two Former Financial Regulators, and Sets the 21st Party Congress for Late 2027

The plenum ended October 29 with a resolution on full and rigorous Party self-governance. It confirmed cases against 34 Central Committee members, expelled 33 including ex-CMC vice chairman Zhang Youxia and ex-CSRC chair Yi Huiman, and stripped former NFRA head Li Yunze of his Party posts. Zhang Shengmin joined the Politburo.


The communique offered no new fiscal or monetary steps. Markets followed the AI-hardware sell-off: the CSI 300 fell 1.03% and the Shenzhen Component 2.92% on the day.

Key signal: The formal purge of two former financial regulators keeps bank and broker supervisors cautious on new products and foreign-partner approvals, and with no economic measures from the plenum, the October PMI and the November 2-6 NPC Standing Committee session carry the near-term policy read.
