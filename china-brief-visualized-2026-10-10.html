STORY 1 · AI & TECH
Manus Raises More Than $500 Million in Its First Round Since Beijing Unwound the Meta Deal, at a Reported $4 Billion Valuation

Butterfly Effect, Manus's parent, said on October 8 it raised more than $500 million, led by Boyu Capital and IDG Capital with Tencent, HSG and ZhenFund. Bloomberg reported a valuation of about $4 billion, roughly twice Meta's $2 billion offer that Beijing ordered unwound in April.


Domestic capital has replaced the US buyer at a higher price. Manus has not disclosed current revenue, and a Hong Kong listing is the exit it is reported to be weighing.

Key signal: Beijing blocked Manus's sale to Meta, and Chinese capital has now refinanced the company at roughly twice Meta's price, so foreign buyers of Chinese-founded AI startups should treat a Hong Kong IPO as the realistic exit.

STORY 2 · AI & TECH
Tencent Weighs Up to $5 Billion in Offshore Bonds, Its Second Big Debt Raise Since June, as AI Capex Runs Ahead of Cash Flow

Tencent is weighing up to $5 billion of offshore bonds in dollars and offshore yuan, after a $4.66 billion deal in June. Q2 capex rose 176% to ¥52.8 billion and free cash flow fell to minus ¥13.8 billion.


Excluding compute prepayments, free cash flow would have been ¥37.6 billion, so the debt funds chips bought ahead. Earnings cover the bonds easily. Q3 results in mid-November need to show AI revenue that justifies the spend.

Key signal: Tencent is turning to the bond market because Q2 capex of ¥52.8 billion pushed free cash flow to minus ¥13.8 billion, which leaves credit risk low and puts the burden on Q3 results to show AI revenue that justifies the spend.

STORY 3 · FINANCE & MACRO
PBOC Rejects Undervaluation Claims as the Yuan's Trade-Weighted Index Reaches 103.9, Its Highest Since April 2022

The PBOC rejected claims the yuan is undervalued and said it has never pursued competitive devaluation, as EU Trade Commissioner Šefčovič held talks in Beijing. The October 9 fixing of 6.7330 was the strongest since February 2023.


Bloomberg's CFETS replica hit 103.9, a four-year high, so the yuan is rising against the euro too. That gives Europe part of what it asks for while the PBOC keeps the decision in its own hands.

Key signal: The PBOC is publicly rejecting EU pressure for a stronger yuan while its fixing pushes the trade-weighted index to a four-year high, so positioning should follow the fixing rather than the rhetoric.
