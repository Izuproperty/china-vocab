Policy timeline: Japan's AI framework build-out
Why now
Deployment targets (10M robots by 2040) outran the legal plumbing. Liability, certification, and data rules for AI in care, transport, and government have to live in sector law, not guidelines.
Investor read
Clarified liability for physical AI would de-risk the robotics and integrator names the Noetra program feeds. Frontier-model restrictions, if they come, bite foreign labs harder than domestic adopters.
Watch item
The council's membership and first work program. Reorganized councils can produce real legislation or consensus management; the first circulated drafts will show which this is.

Current account surplus, monthly (trillion yen)
Composition
The surplus is dominated by primary income — dividends and coupons on overseas assets, much of it reinvested abroad without ever converting to yen. Headline flow overstates yen demand.
Implication
The "yen is too cheap versus fundamentals" case weakens when the fundamentals do not supply the flow. Durable reversal depends on the rate path and repatriation incentives, not the surplus.
Policy calendar
Markets price better-than-even odds of another BOJ hike by October; the July 30-31 meeting and any renewed intervention are the near-term catalysts, not this data.
