STORY 1 · AI & TECH · AI GOVERNANCE / COMPUTE
China's Data Regulator Will Write National Standards for Metering and Billing AI Tokens, With Daily Use Past 500 Trillion

The National Data Administration (国家数据局) said on Nov 21 it will set standards for metering (计量), billing (计费) and quality-testing AI tokens (词元). Director Liu Liehong (刘烈宏) also wants to curb "low-quality duplication" among token factories (词元工厂).


Carriers sell token packages, Inner Mongolia runs a token exchange, and Beijing's 2026-2028 plan rates factories on throughput and efficiency. Common rules would make tokens from different sellers comparable on price and quality.

Key signal: Standards framed around curbing duplication could squeeze small resellers of rented compute and lift floor prices, which would narrow the cost gap that draws foreign developers to Chinese models.

STORY 2 · AI & TECH · CHIPS / EXPORT CONTROLS
WSJ Names 52-Year-Old Chinese National Li Ming as the Operator of Megaspeed, a Singapore Reseller That Arranged Nearly $12 Billion of Nvidia Orders for Chinese Buyers

The WSJ says Li Ming ran Megaspeed and Alice Huang was its public face. Li is on bail in Singapore (新加坡) on fraud charges over servers that went to Malaysia (马来西亚).


A successor, Novagate Cloud, received at least $300 million of Nvidia (英伟达) hardware in Malaysia in Jan-May 2026. Bessent said Nvidia has not explained who ran export-control (出口管制) compliance under Jensen Huang (黄仁勋).

Key signal: Southeast Asian data centres were a main route to Nvidia hardware for Chinese buyers; the Nov 19 remote-access rule targets that route, which pushes more Chinese training onto domestic chips.

STORY 3 · FINANCE · FISCAL POLICY
China Ran Its First Monthly Budget Surplus in a Year in October, ¥265.7 Billion ($39.8 Billion), as Income Tax Jumped and Investment Kept Falling

Fiscal revenue (财政收入) rose 4.9% to ¥19.57 trillion ($2.93 trillion) in Jan-Oct; spending rose 1% to ¥22.8 trillion ($3.42 trillion). Personal income tax (个人所得税) rose 22.9%.


October income tax of ¥296 billion ($44.4 billion) included over ¥120 billion ($18.0 billion) from the offshore-trust tax. The broad budget ran a surplus (财政盈余) while investment fell an estimated 17%.

Key signal: A monthly surplus during a double-digit investment slump is fiscal tightening; the December Central Economic Work Conference (中央经济工作会议) sets whether the Ministry spends its remaining room.
