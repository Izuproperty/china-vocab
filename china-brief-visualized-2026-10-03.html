STORY 1 · AI & TECH
Huawei's Mate 90 Ships a Folded Kirin Chip, and Richard Yu Says China's Advanced Chip Capacity Is Still Tight

Huawei began selling the Mate 90 on October 1 at 5,999 to 12,999 yuan. Premium models use the Kirin 9050 Pro, a LogicFolding chip Huawei rates at 238 million transistors per mm², with 31% higher system performance and 140% faster on-device LLM inference. The node is undisclosed; analysts place it on SMIC's 7nm-class N+2 or N+3.


Richard Yu said China's advanced chip capacity "remains very limited." Kirin and Ascend share the same SMIC wafers, so strong phone demand competes directly with AI accelerator output.

Key signal: Huawei is using chip architecture to compensate for blocked lithography. Yield and cost per die are undisclosed, and a hot phone cycle could slow Ascend deliveries to Chinese cloud buyers.

STORY 2 · AI & TECH
A $300 Million Smuggling Arrest Puts Nvidia's Southeast Asia Sales Under Senate Scrutiny

US authorities arrested Greg Lui on October 1 over more than $300 million of Nvidia-equipped servers allegedly sent to China in 2023-24. Bloomberg reported that Bangkok's OBON received about $2.5 billion of AI servers without the ~100MW needed to run them, and officials estimate 115,000 restricted Nvidia processors are already in China.


Senators Banks and Warren want Nvidia's export licenses reviewed. Nvidia says a friendly-country startup's growth plan "is an opportunity for America, not a red flag."

Key signal: A license review would tighten checks on Southeast Asian buyers, shrinking a grey-market channel Chinese labs use and pushing more training onto Huawei's supply-limited Ascend chips.

STORY 3 · FINANCE & MACRO
Golden Week Opens With 329.5 Million Trips, Down 1.9%, as Rail Gains and Private Car Travel Falls

The Ministry of Transport counted 329.5 million cross-regional trips on October 1, down 1.9%. Rail rose 9.0% and commercial road 8.5%, while private car trips fell 4.5%. The ministry expects about 2.13 billion trips over the holiday.


Last year's Golden Week spend per trip was 911.04 yuan, a three-year low. More households are going abroad this year (54% plan outbound trips vs 35%), and the holiday is a day shorter than 2025's eight-day break.

Key signal: Volume opened flat. Per-trip spending against last year's 911-yuan low will decide whether the holiday gives consumer stocks a lift when mainland markets reopen.
