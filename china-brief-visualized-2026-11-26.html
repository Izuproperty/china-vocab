STORY 1 · AI & TECH · HUMANOID ROBOTICS / AUTONOMOUS DRIVING
XPeng Puts Its Car and Robot Brains on One "Turing" Stack at AI Day and Sets a Q2 2027 Public Release for the IRON Humanoid

XPeng (小鹏汽车) used its Nov 25 AI Day in Guangzhou to put cars and the IRON humanoid (人形机器人) on one Turing chip and VLA (视觉语言动作模型) stack. IRON has 76 degrees of freedom, 1,500+ tactile sensors (触觉传感器) and 2,250 TOPS of on-board compute; showroom training starts in Q1 2027 and public release is targeted for Q2.


On cars, XPeng reports a 13.93% fall in accident claims since VLA 2.0 and a 12.4% range gain in congestion, with the first OTA in December. Its robotics unit raised $900 million+ in August at a $6.3 billion+ valuation. He Xiaopeng (何小鹏) calls it embodied AI (具身智能) that learns through a body.

Key signal: Sharing one chip and model stack spreads R&D cost across a car business with volume and a robot business with no revenue; the December OTA and a priced Q2 2027 IRON launch are the checkpoints, and the safety and claims data are XPeng's own.

STORY 2 · AI & TECH · FOUNDATION MODELS / COMPUTE
Maybank Says the US Remote-Access Rule Splits China's AI Labs Into Chip Owners and Renters, With Moonshot and MiniMax Most Exposed

A Maybank Investment Bank note (Nov 24) ranks Chinese AI labs by exposure to the US rule on remote access (远程访问) to advanced chips, effective Nov 19. Moonshot (月之暗面) and MiniMax (稀宇科技) top the list because they lack in-house chips; Moonshot's 2.8-trillion-parameter Kimi exceeds an eight-card H200 server.


Chip owners hold up better: Alibaba has shipped 560,000+ Zhenwu accelerators, Baidu's accelerator subscriptions rose 128% in Q3 and Z.AI runs on 100,000+ domestic chips. Huawei's 2026 output is estimated at 880,000 H100-equivalents, under 4% of Nvidia's 23 million, with a ~73x HBM (高带宽内存) gap.

Key signal: The rule closes the offshore rented-compute (租用算力) route, which raises capital needs for independent labs weighing IPOs and favours platforms that own their compute (算力); Southeast Asian data-centre licences or a large domestic chip allocation to an independent would change this.

STORY 3 · FINANCE · EQUITY MARKETS
STAR 50 Falls 5.1% and CATL Drops Below ¥1 Trillion ($149 Billion) in Value as a Chip-Led Rout Hits A-Shares

On Nov 25 the STAR 50 (科创板) fell 5.12%, ChiNext (创业板) 4.19% and the Shanghai Composite 2.03% to 3,476.47, with turnover (成交额) down to ¥1.38 trillion ($206 billion). Semiconductor (半导体) names led: Hua Hong lost 13%+ and NAURA 9.55%.


CATL (宁德时代) fell 8.33% to ¥208.42 ($31.06), taking its market value (市值) below ¥1 trillion ($149 billion). The selling was regional, with the KOSPI down 6.16%, and a pending US excess-capacity probe and a Jan 10 truce expiry added China-specific trade risk.

Key signal: This month's margin and fee relief lowers the cost of leverage but does not address AI hardware earnings; thin turnover, the Nov 30 PMI and the US capacity findings will show whether this is a crowded-trade correction or a wider de-rating.
