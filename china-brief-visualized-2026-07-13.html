Quota Burn-Down · A 2024–26 Program Finishing More Than a Year Early
Core Claim
The 化债huà zhài · debt resolution program is finishing over a year early. Without a successor, H2 2026 sees passive fiscal tightening exactly as reflation is being tested — the ending of stimulus by completion rather than decision.
Unverified
The 94% figure is Bloomberg's compilation of issuance data, not an official milestone. Beijing has let programs lapse before without replacement, and nothing obliges the late-July Politburo to address it explicitly.
Actionable Signal
Watch the MOF H1 fiscal briefing and the Politburo readout for successor language. A shift of borrowing toward the central government balance sheet — not another 专项债zhuānxiàng zhài · special-purpose bond tranche — is what the bond market would treat as regime change.
