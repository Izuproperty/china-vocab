TEL Late-July Earnings: Primary Readthrough
Tokyo Electron's Q1 FY2027 results will expose equipment order velocity at Rapidus IIM-1 (Chitose), JASM Kumamoto, and Micron Hiroshima HBM fab — the three most significant Japan semiconductor construction sites currently in ramp phase.
Kioxia Gen.10: Yield is the Thesis Test
BiCS Flash Gen.10 (332-layer, 1Tb TLC) samples target July-August 2026. If initial yield quality supports Micron-scale margins, Kioxia's 48x net profit guidance for April-June becomes a durable structural level, not a one-quarter peak.
Rapidus H2 Design Window
Tenstorrent remains the only confirmed tape-out customer for Rapidus 2nm. A named hyperscaler or sovereign AI institute customer by September/October is the condition METI's supplementary budget language will implicitly reference.

First Acceleration in 8 Months
Core-core rose to 1.9% in June from 1.6% in May, reversing several months of softening. Tokyo CPI leads national CPI by 3-4 weeks — June national print likely confirms the same inflection when released in late July.
October Hike Now Baseline
Tamura's explicit "every few months" cadence (June 25) combined with 1.9% core-core positions October's MPM as the default next-move date. July MPM (30-31) is likely a hold with hawkish guidance revision.
Yen Stuck Despite Data
USD/JPY held 161.6 on the CPI beat — the 300bp+ rate differential with the US Federal Funds rate (~3.75%) outweighs domestic inflation signals. Yen normalization requires Fed cuts or a BOJ cadence that markets believe in, not just one data point.
