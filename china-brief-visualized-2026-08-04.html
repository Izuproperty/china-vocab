STORY 1 · AI & TECH / FOUNDATION MODELS
Third Frontier Release in Three Weeks: Alibaba Ships Qwen3.8-Max, Claims an Edge Over Fable 5 on Its Own Scorecard

Alibaba moved Qwen3.8-Max from preview to production on August 3: a 2.4T-parameter mixture-of-experts model activating ~95B params per request, multimodal, 1M-token context. Alibaba's own benchmark table shows it ahead of Claude Fable 5 on PaperBench and IFBench, and ahead of Fable 5 but behind GPT-5.6 Sol on Terminal-Bench 2.1.


Every comparison figure comes from Alibaba's own table — no third-party benchmark run was cited at launch, the same pattern as Kimi K3 and GLM-5.2 this cycle. Notably, at 2.4T total / ~95B active, it's smaller than Moonshot's 2.8T Kimi K3 — a signal Alibaba is competing on inference cost as much as raw scale.

Key signal: Three Chinese labs have now shipped frontier-class models within a month. Price and efficiency are doing as much work as benchmark scores in determining adoption — watch for independent replication before treating any of these claims as settled.

STORY 2 · AI & TECH / PHYSICAL AI & ROBOTICS
From Craftsman's Hands to Training Data: Inside the Guangzhou Glove Teaching Robots to Feel

Xinhua profiled Guangzhou's Li-Gong Industrial, whose data-capture kit — binocular camera, wrist camera, 100+-point tactile glove — converts skilled-worker hand movements into robot-manipulation training data; orders booked through October. Separately, Shenzhen's 1024 Robotics runs autonomous logistics robots on Metro Line 16 via a local "scenario forest" siting policy.


MIIT data cited puts China at 400+ humanoid models (over half the global total) and ~70% of global quadruped sales; one industry estimate has the embodied-intelligence market growing from ¥213.3B (2018) to ¥1.09T (2026e), 22–23% CAGR. All vendor/association-sourced, standard for this stage — but the throughline is that manipulation-training infrastructure is becoming its own sub-industry, not a side effect of humanoid hardware.

Key signal: The data/training-infrastructure layer (gloves, motion capture, siting policy) is a less crowded trade than humanoid hardware itself — worth tracking Tier 2 names like Li-Gong alongside Unitree and AgiBot.

STORY 3 · FINANCE & MACRO / FISCAL POLICY
Same Week, Different Lever: NDRC Fast-Tracks ¥800 Billion in Policy Financing a Day After the PBOC's Rate Hold

NDRC said July 31 it is accelerating ¥800B in policy-backed financial instruments through Q3, while drafting a domestic-demand strategy covering employment, smart-device consumption, private investment and infrastructure REITs — with computing-power buildout flagged as a private-investment lever worth an estimated ¥4T over the 15th Five-Year Plan.


This follows the July 30 Politburo's call to accelerate fiscal spending and lands one day after the PBOC's own "timely tools" pledge (Issue #76). Fiscal policy is the lever actually moving; monetary policy stays in a holding pattern. The ¥800B figure is faster disbursement of existing quota, not incremental stimulus.

Key signal: Watch for Q3 disbursement data confirming the acceleration, and the full domestic-demand strategy once published — neither is available yet, so the announcement is a direction-of-travel signal, not a completed policy.
