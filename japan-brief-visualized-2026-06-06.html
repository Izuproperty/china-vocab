Funding Structure

IPA rounds are equity-equivalent injections through a state agency — not grant subsidies. They strengthen Rapidus's balance sheet without creating near-term debt obligations.

Private Sector Signal

The 32-company private co-investment (¥167.6B) includes all major keiretsu anchors. The list's composition is strategic rather than purely financial — government alignment, not demand signals.

Key Risk: Customers

No external supply commitment has been disclosed. IBM provided process IP but not wafer volume. Rapidus needs hyperscaler or sovereign AI anchor customers to validate the demand side by 2025.
