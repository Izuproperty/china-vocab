Two-Way Capital Screen — How Decree 837 Pairs With Inbound Controls
Core Claim
First State Council regulation dedicated to 境外投资jìngwài tóuzī · outbound investment: lifecycle supervision, 安全审查ānquán shěnchá · security review, and forced divestiture power replace one-time approval.
Unverified

Investable Signal
