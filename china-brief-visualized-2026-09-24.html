STORY 1 · AI & TECH
Beijing Opens Its Own Investigation Into DeepSeek and Moonshot Over the Anthropic Routing Claims

China's Cyberspace Administration (CAC) is investigating DeepSeek and Moonshot over whether routing user requests to Claude — the behavior Anthropic alleged on September 10 — violated China's Data Security Law. CAC summoned all seven labs named in Anthropic's report before narrowing to these two.


Shares of Z.AI, MiniMax, and Alibaba all fell on the report, even though Alibaba isn't under active investigation. The market priced in sector-wide read-across risk before any finding was published.

Key signal: A data-security review this week establishes scrutiny, not an outcome — CAC probes have run for months before without public resolution.

STORY 2 · AI & TECH · ROBOTICS
A Turnkey Platform for Humanoid Robots: ALTER and Foxconn Register Their Manufacturing Venture

Auto-engineering firm ALTER and Foxconn registered Beijing Futekang Intelligent Robotics on September 10, operationalizing a "robot turnkey" platform unveiled in July that offers humanoid-robot makers full-stack design-to-production services.


The bet is that automotive manufacturing discipline transfers to robots — addressing the same production bottleneck regulators cited this week in slowing new robotics IPO approvals.

Key signal: Registration sets up the legal entity, not a proven track record — no customer, timeline, or revenue split has been disclosed yet.

STORY 3 · FINANCE & MACRO
Sixteen Months Without a Move: PBOC Holds Rates Steady as the Yuan Strengthens Into the Summit

The PBOC held the LPR unchanged on September 20 for a 16th straight month (1-year: 3.00%, 5-year: 3.50%), with all 21 surveyed economists expecting no change, against a backdrop of resilient exports and accelerating industrial output.


The currency moved more than the rate: the yuan strengthened through September, aligning with He Lifeng's remarks ahead of today's Trump-Xi meeting and Beijing's 2026 pattern of using the exchange rate rather than rate cuts to project stability around sensitive diplomatic dates.

Key signal: Whether the yuan's strength persists after this week's summit — not the LPR hold itself — is the signal to watch for policy intent.
