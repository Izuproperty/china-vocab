The New Three-Tier Regime for Data-Center Builds
Risk
东数西算dōng shù xī suàn · Eastern Data, Western Compute

The 2022 initiative encouraged provinces to compete on capacity — and they overbuilt. China has issued compute-discipline guidance before; interprovincial incentives have repeatedly outrun central intent.

Watch
算力suànlì · computing power

The rule bites only if enforcement is visible: a subsidy clawback, a grid-access denial, or a public project rejection. Until then it signals where capacity is meant to flow, not a completed reallocation.

Signal
数据中心shùjù zhōngxīn · data center

If enforcement holds, demand tilts toward national-hub operators, state integrators and hub-designated domestic accelerator suppliers — away from the long tail of local vanity builds.

Risk

Efficiency is unverified: effective training FLOPs per watt and per dollar versus an equivalent Nvidia deployment, and job-failure rates, are unknown. A self-sufficiency showcase is not parity.

Watch

The hard part is the training toolchain, not peak FLOPs. Signal to watch: whether frontier labs with Nvidia access voluntarily move training onto the Ascend + MindSpore stack — not just an Entity-Listed one.

Risk
净息差jìng xīchā · net interest margin

Margin compression is structural, not cyclical. Thin spreads leave banks less able to absorb bad loans — and a reported ~1.5% NPL ratio sits against independent estimates nearer 10%.

Watch
直接融资zhíjiē róngzī · direct financing

The PBOC frames the slowdown as a shift to direct financing — but ~83% of that is government bonds, ~70% held by banks. It's fiscal policy through the bond market, not a maturing capital market.

Signal
社会融资规模shèhuì róngzī guīmó · total social financing

Rate cuts have lost traction, shifting H2 support onto fiscal channels. Watch credit and TSF prints as a more honest read on domestic demand than headline GDP, which leaned on AI exports.
