STORY 1 · AI & TECH · ROBOTICS
Noetix Says Its ¥9,998 ($1,500) Bumi Topped JD's Humanoid Charts Over Singles' Day, While Only About 8% of China's Humanoids Go Into Production Work

Noetix Robotics (松延动力) says Bumi (小布米) ranked first on JD.com (京东) by units and value from Oct 9 to Nov 11. JD says humanoid (人形机器人) sales rose 700% year on year. Most buyers are hobbyists, developers and creators, and consumer-grade (消费级) buyers were about 60% to 65% of sales at 618.


Margins are thin. Founder Jiang Zheyuan (姜哲源) calls Bumi's gross margin (毛利率) low and expects break-even in 2027. Swancor made 10,000 Qiyuan robots but posted a nine-month net loss of ¥422 million ($63 million).

Key signal: Sub-¥30,000 ($4,500) humanoids sell in volume as hobby kits, but each unit brings little revenue, and only about 8% of shipments do production work.

STORY 2 · AI & TECH · GENERATIVE AI / MEDIA
Bona's Fully AI-Generated Feature "Sanxingdui: Future Past" Takes About $270,000 in Three Weeks Against a $1.5 Million Budget, as AI Micro-Drama Stocks Rise 25%

Bona Film Group (博纳影业) spent about $1.5 million on a feature set at the Sanxingdui (三星堆) site and made fully with AI. Its box office (票房) reached about $181,000 in the opening days and about $270,000 after three weeks.


AI micro-dramas (短剧) are selling well. More than 95% of Q1 titles were AI-generated, and AI micro-drama stocks rose over 25% in early November.

Key signal: Viewers accept cheap generative AI (生成式人工智能) in phone-length episodes but would not pay cinema prices for it.

STORY 3 · FINANCE · MARKET REGULATION / WEEK AHEAD
CSRC's Derivatives Rules Take Effect Today, Requiring ¥500 Million ($75 Million) of Net Capital to Deal Swaps and OTC Options, as October Activity Data Lands With Retail Sales Seen at 0.8%

The CSRC's derivatives (衍生品) measures take effect today. Only securities and futures firms with at least ¥500 million ($75 million) of net capital (净资本) can deal OTC options (场外期权) and total return swaps (收益互换). Insiders can no longer trade derivatives on their own company's shares.


October activity data also lands today, and consensus sees retail sales slowing to 0.8%. The loan prime rate (贷款市场报价利率) fixes on Friday, with no change expected.

Key signal: New dealing moves to larger brokers and insiders lose a private hedging route, so more stake sales may come through disclosed block trades.
