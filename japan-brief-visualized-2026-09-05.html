STORY 1 · AI & TECH
Japan's AI Data Center Capacity Set to Quadruple by 2033
A Nikkei survey found Japan's AI data-center capacity on track to more than quadruple by 2033 on roughly $60bn of combined investment. NTT Data alone plans ~750MW of new capacity (¥1.5tn / $9.6bn outlay); Blackstone has pledged $30bn separately. NTT shares fell about 1% as investors weighed the capital intensity.
Japan AI Data Center Buildout: ~$60bn Combined Investment
Investor Takeaway
Demand looks resilient (100MW+ of inbound inferencing inquiries), but grid approvals in constrained hubs like Inzai run 8-10 years — watch whether committed capex converts to contracted, grid-connected revenue on schedule.
SIGNAL
Global cloud providers (AWS, Microsoft) already drive 80%+ of NTT Data's Japan data-center revenue — capacity leases out almost as fast as it's built.
WATCH
NTT Data's 100MW Tochigi facility breaks ground next year — the first major test of whether grid connection keeps pace with capex.
RISK
Deepstone Partners' CEO warns the ¥1.5tn outlay could pressure NTT shares against deeper-pocketed global rivals; shares already -1%.
STORY 2 · SEMICONDUCTORS
ASML to Grow Japan Workforce 40% by 2030
ASML Japan will grow its domestic headcount from ~500 to ~700 by 2030 to support Rapidus and TSMC's Kumamoto expansion. The Hokkaido team supporting Rapidus has already grown from 24 to 50 engineers since 2024; Kumamoto is likely next as TSMC builds its second JASM fab there.
ASML Japan Headcount Growth: National vs. Hokkaido (Rapidus) Team
Investor Takeaway
As the sole EUV supplier, ASML's hiring plan is a leading indicator for which Japan fabs are genuinely on track — worth cross-checking against Rapidus's 2027 mass-production timeline and TSMC's Kumamoto Fab 2 schedule.
SIGNAL
ASML's Japan sales reached ~$1.4bn in 2025; Rapidus, TSMC, and Micron's Hiroshima facility are its named domestic customers.
WATCH
Kumamoto staffing plans — TSMC's second JASM fab and the Aug 11 Sony image-sensor JV both point to added ASML presence there.
RISK
This is pre-positioning ahead of demand, not confirmation — Rapidus still has no confirmed volume customer for 2027 output.
STORY 3 · MACRO & BOJ
Yen Jumps 2% as Carry-Trade Unwind Meets Hawkish BOJ Signals
The yen jumped over 2% to a one-month high of ¥155.28 as traders unwound yen-funded carry trades following hawkish comments from Governor Ueda and board member Takata on a possible larger-than-usual hike at the Sept 17-18 meeting. FX intervention chatter returned for the first time since late July.
USD/JPY: Carry-Trade Unwind, Late Aug → Sept 4
Investor Takeaway
Hike odds barely moved (~74% → ~73%) even as the yen itself moved sharply — this is a positioning/carry-trade story, not a fresh repricing of the BOJ's reaction function. Watch how orderly any post-hike move is, not just whether the hike comes.
SIGNAL
The currency itself moved 2%+, not just rate expectations — a sharper, faster adjustment than the gradual repricing seen through August.
WATCH
US nonfarm payrolls (Sept 5) is the next data point that could shift relative Fed-BOJ rate math before the Sept 17-18 meeting.
RISK
MOF intervention chatter is back for the first time since late July — a disorderly unwind, not just the hike itself, is now a live risk.
