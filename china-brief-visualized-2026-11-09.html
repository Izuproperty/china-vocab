STORY 1 · AI & TECH · ROBOTICS / CONSUMER EMBODIED AI
Swancor's Qiyuan Rolls Its 10,000th Personal Robot Off the Line, 14 Days After the 5,000th, and Reports a 98% Monthly Active Rate

Qiyuan Robotics (启元机器人), the consumer brand of Swancor (上纬新材, 688585), said its 10,000th personal robot (个人机器人) came off the line on November 8, 14 days after the 5,000th. Mass production (量产) started in October, with daily capacity ramping to 240. Co-president Liu Dongfang reported a monthly active rate (月活率) above 98% and three hours of daily use.


The Q1 humanoid and T1 transformer start at ¥19,999 ($3,000). At base price 10,000 units come to about ¥200 million ($30 million), close to the ¥210 million ($31 million) of robot prepayments Swancor reported at mid-year.

Key signal: Qiyuan has a disclosed consumer volume that most humanoid makers lack, but the counts are production, not deliveries, and the implied 357 units a day exceeds the 240-unit capacity cited, so Q4 revenue recognition is the check.

STORY 2 · AI & TECH · FOUNDATION MODELS
Alibaba Puts an Unnamed "Stealth" Model, Qoduck, Into Its Qoder Coding Tool for Free a Day After Teasing Qwen4, as the Launch Itself Slips Past Its Hinted Week

Alibaba (阿里巴巴) added Qoduck, a stealth model (隐身模型) of undisclosed origin, to its Qoder coding assistant (编程助手) on November 7. Individual plans get a free daily allowance and half-price overage. Users report a 1 million-token context window (上下文窗口) and a Qwen-family tokenizer.


A November 6 Qwen (通义千问) teaser left a blank readers filled with 鲲 (kūn). The launch hinted for the week after October 29 has not happened. Leaked, unconfirmed scores put a Qwen4-Flash ahead of Claude Opus 5.5 on one test at about a sixth of the cost per task.

Key signal: Alibaba is gathering paid developer usage before publishing a model card or licence; whether Qwen4 ships with open weights above MOFCOM's draft 2-trillion-parameter threshold is the open question for the launch.

STORY 3 · FINANCE & MACRO · WEEK-AHEAD PREVIEW
Week Ahead: October CPI and PPI Land Today With Forecasts Near 0.8% and 5.2%, and Brokers See Exports Up 38% in Tuesday's Trade Data

NBS (国家统计局) releases October CPI (居民消费价格指数) and PPI (工业生产者出厂价格指数) at 09:30 Beijing today. September CPI was 0.9% and PPI 5.0%, the fastest since June 2022. Brokers expect CPI of 0.7% to 0.9% and PPI of 5.1% to 5.4%, with pork prices pulling CPI down and crude holding PPI up.


Trade data follow Tuesday: Nomura sees exports up 38% and a $126.1 billion surplus, Citi a $112.8 billion surplus. MOF sells up to ¥4 billion ($600 million) of green sovereign bonds (绿色主权债券) in Canada, and October credit data are due around November 13.

Key signal: A scissors gap (剪刀差) above four points shows energy costs hitting producers without reaching households, which squeezes downstream margins; a PPI above 5.4% would make the PBOC likelier to hold its 1.25% overnight rate.
