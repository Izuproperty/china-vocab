STORY 1 · AI & TECH Nikkei Asia
Japan's Top IT Integrators Set 2030 Target for AI-Led Development — Splitting Into Rival OpenAI and Anthropic Camps
Fujitsu, NEC, Hitachi, NTT Data, and SoftBank are converging on a 2030 target to shift from people-led to AI-led software development, using generative coding agents to compress the traditional build pipeline. The industry has split into two camps: NTT Data/SoftBank betting on OpenAI for scale, and Fujitsu/NEC/Hitachi betting on Anthropic for a security-first "Japanese-style AI" positioning aimed at regulated clients.
Investor read-through: This is a margin and headcount story more than a technology story — watch entry-level engineering hiring at Japan's largest IT employers, and whether FSA-regulated clients (banks, insurers, government) gravitate toward the Anthropic camp's reliability framing in RFPs.

STORY 2 · SEMI & INDUSTRIAL METI
Japan's Third Round of Chip-Equipment Export Controls Takes Effect, Closing the Advanced-Packaging Loophole
METI's May 29 revision to the FEFTA control list took effect August 1, extending export licensing to cover the full advanced-packaging equipment chain for the first time — closing a gap Chinese fabs had used to compensate for restricted front-end lithography access. Tokyo Electron, SCREEN Holdings, Disco, and Kokusai Electric are most exposed.
Investor read-through: China is still roughly a third of Tokyo Electron's revenue base; the first two rounds already cut combined China sales at TEL/SCREEN/Kokusai by close to 20% YoY. Watch Q3 China revenue disclosures for how much of the remaining business this round closes off.

STORY 3 · MACRO & BOJ Reuters / CNBC
Japan's Wholesale Inflation Eases to 7.2% But Stays Elevated, Keeping a September BOJ Hike Alive
July producer prices rose 7.2% YoY, undershooting the 7.4% consensus but still near a three-decade high, driven largely by electricity costs. The unusual part is the gap to consumer inflation (1.9% headline, 1.6–2.0% core) — attributed to Takaichi-cabinet energy subsidies shielding households from the wholesale pass-through.
Investor read-through: Elevated pipeline inflation keeps Ueda's "as soon as September" hike case alive, but the subsidy-suppressed CPI print gives the Board room to wait for the Aug 21 and Sep 18 CPI checkpoints before committing to a date.
