STORY 1 · AI & TECH
Agents Run on CPUs Too: Alibaba's T-Head Puts Its Next Yitian Server Chips on a 2027 Roadmap

T-Head set out Yitian 720 and 730 server CPUs for 2027 and a Yitian 750 on in-house cores. Alibaba Cloud added the 400G Panmai 920 network card and the Lingjun Zhenwu M890 supernode.


Alibaba's case: agents loop through reasoning and tool calls, tool execution takes about 60% of response time, and 41% of iteration steps cannot be parallelized, so CPUs, memory and networking set the pace.

Key signal: Agent-era capex tilts toward CPUs and networking, and Alibaba wants to supply that layer itself. The figures are self-reported and the chips arrive in 2027.

STORY 2 · AI & TECH
996 AI Exhibitors, Three Times Last Year: Hangzhou's Digital Trade Expo Starts Counting AI as an Export

The fifth Global Digital Trade Expo closed September 27 with 996 AI exhibitors among 2,008, visits up 67% to 428,000 and more than 12,000 foreign buyers.


Hangzhou booked ¥254.1 billion of digital trade in January to August and reports AI exports up more than 150%, without defining the category or its base.

Key signal: Local governments now count AI as export revenue, which places Chinese AI inside the trade-surplus debate.

STORY 3 · FINANCE & MACRO
The Zero-Yuan Phones Were Loans: China's Three Carriers Halt Handset Credit Days Before New Marketing Rules Start

China Mobile, China Telecom and China Unicom stopped new financed-handset contracts on September 24. Buyers had signed loans bundled into phone plans, and missed bills hit their credit records.


Online financial-marketing rules from the PBOC, MIIT and six other regulators take effect September 30 and ban misleading financial marketing.

Key signal: Enforcement is starting with credit hidden inside non-financial brands, so checkout financing on other platforms faces the same test.
