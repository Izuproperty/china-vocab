Watch the financing mix
Neocloud comparables have repriced sharply on debt-load concerns. How much of SB Neo sits on SoftBank Corp.'s balance sheet versus project debt is the real disclosure to watch in the July filing.
Anchor customers unnamed
The 10 GW case requires hyperscaler anchor tenants before fiscal 2027 launch. None have been named. The incorporation window is the first chance to see whether any surface.
Noetra's roster is a signal
Roughly 44 companies are expected to join the METI-commissioned consortium. Whether Fujitsu and Rakuten commit is a read on how corporates handicap a state-anchored model against building in-house.

GCAP Program Timeline
Filled indigo marker: this week's trigger event. Sources: Japan Times, Defense News, July 3-4, 2026.
Workshare starts binding
A funded design phase turns the org chart into engineering commitments: IHI-Rolls-Royce on propulsion, Mitsubishi Electric on avionics and sensing, MHI on airframe integration.
First export-scale line item
Japanese defense revenue has been driven by the domestic buildup toward 2% of GDP. GCAP is the first program with genuine third-country sales potential under the loosened export regime.
Schedule risk is the base case
Trilateral programs rarely hold schedule, and 2035 assumes a compressed cycle for a sixth-generation platform. Each partner's fiscal politics can stall the program independently.

May wages are the test
The first wage print since the June hike. A real-wage reading above 1% confirms the income momentum the rate path assumes; a soft print pushes the next-hike debate to year-end.
Read the Sakura anecdotes
The July Regional Economic Report is due this week. April's edition flagged smaller firms lagging the 5%+ headline settlements — whether pass-through is broadening is the key judgment.
Sequencing, not direction
A hawkish surprise supports the yen but aggravates the fiscal worries behind the long-end selloff, with the 10-year JGB already at a three-decade-high yield. The data decides the script.
