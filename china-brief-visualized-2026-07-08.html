The Policy Runway: June Campaign to Year-End Deployment Target
Core Claim
An MIIT official put a six-figure humanoid output forecast on the record ten days before WAIC — a deliberate demand-side signal to the supply chain and capital markets. But 整机产量zhěngjī chǎnliàng · complete-unit output counts education and research units, not economically productive robots.
Unverified
No breakdown of the 100,000 units by use case or maker was disclosed. With 140+ domestic humanoid firms, a price war remains the sector's most predictable risk; the ~10,000-unit commercial-deployment target is the honest adoption measure.
Actionable Signal
The forecast supports the component and contract-manufacturing thesis — actuators, reducers, sensors — more than any single robot maker. Watch WAIC for order announcements and the Unitree listing as sentiment catalysts for 具身智能jùshēn zhìnéng · embodied AI supply chains.

US Companies' Token Share Routed to Chinese Models (OpenRouter)
Core Claim
This is revealed preference by paying US customers, not benchmark theater. The driver is 性价比xìngjiàbǐ · price-performance: tasks that don't need the best model get routed to the cheapest one that clears the bar — and Chinese 开源kāiyuán · open source models are winning that trade.
Unverified
OpenRouter skews to developers and startups; large regulated enterprises contract directly with US labs, and compliance objections to Chinese-origin models remain hard constraints in the highest-spend sectors. Sustained 30%+ share also invites a Washington policy response.
Actionable Signal
Watch three things: whether share holds after US labs reprice mid-tier models, whether a large US enterprise publicly standardizes on a Chinese open-weight model, and whether Zhipu's GLM-5.2 momentum (27x tokens, 80x customers in week one) persists past the novelty window.

The Buying Streak Accelerates — and Gold Is Still Under 10% of Reserves
Core Claim
Twenty months of buying, accelerating into price swings rather than pausing, is structural reserve policy: a 去美元化qù měiyuán huà · de-dollarization hedge against sanctions risk and dollar-asset concentration — not tactical trading.
Unverified
Monthly SAFE prints are disclosed policy, and actual accumulation may differ — the World Gold Council has repeatedly estimated unreported official buying well above published volumes. The –$26B reserves dip is attributed to valuation effects, not confirmed intervention.
Actionable Signal
With 黄金储备huángjīn chǔbèi · gold reserves under 10% of the portfolio versus a majority at Western peers, the program has years of runway — a persistent structural bid under gold. Watch H2 pace and Q3 Treasury-holdings data.
