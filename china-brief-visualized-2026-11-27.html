STORY 1 · AI & TECH · FOUNDATION MODELS / COMPUTE
Z.AI Pushes GLM-5.5 Into 2027 and Moonshot's Next Kimi May Take Twice as Long as US Curbs Cut Off Rented Compute and Training Data

Reuters (Nov 26) reports Z.AI (智谱) has pushed GLM-5.5, about 3 trillion parameters, from November to Q1 2027 or later. Moonshot (月之暗面) could see its next large model (大模型) cycle stretch from about 6 months to 12+, pointing to mid-2027 for Kimi K4. Z.AI's 750B GLM-5.4 shipped Monday as a stopgap.


Causes: the US remote-access rule (Nov 19), Nvidia's halt to Southeast Asia shipments, and tighter anti-distillation (反蒸馏) defences at OpenAI and Anthropic. Moonshot is distilling (模型蒸馏) from Google and Meta models, renting in Europe and approaching Chinese chipmakers; Z.AI has partly switched on a domestic-chip data centre.

Key signal: The rules now cost renting labs months per flagship, which matters for Moonshot's planned Q1 2027 Hong Kong IPO of up to $5 billion; the report relies on unnamed sources, and Google or Meta could close the distillation workaround.

STORY 2 · AI & TECH · HUMANOID ROBOTICS
EngineAI Opens Its Robot Brain and Motion Models to Developers and Pledges at Least ¥1 Billion ($149 Million) of Partner Support, but Discloses No Orders

EngineAI (众擎机器人) held its first developer conference in Shenzhen on Nov 25. CEO Zhao Tongyang (赵同阳) pledged at least ¥1 billion ($149 million) of partner support with Henan Investment Group and other funds, and said the company will open-source (开源) its whole-body model, BFM (行为基座模型) weights, a benchmark and some training data.


Awaken-HI pretraining data rose from 5,000 to 50,000 hours and real-robot task success from 56.7% to 90%, per the CTO. EngineAI cites 6,000+ GPUs and capacity for tens of thousands of units, and was valued above ¥10 billion ($1.49 billion) in its Series B. It disclosed no orders or shipments.

Key signal: Funding partners and opening models is a platform play; it needs named partners and delivery figures to hold, while sector capacity is running far ahead of shipments.

STORY 3 · FINANCE · TRADE POLICY
USTR Proposes Up to 20% Overcapacity Tariffs on China, With a 40% Alternative, and MOFCOM Calls It Unilateralism

On Nov 25 USTR (美国贸易代表办公室) found 14 of 16 economies act unreasonably on overcapacity (产能过剩) under Section 301 (301条款). China faces up to 20% on top of 2018 duties, up to 10% on Annex A goods, about $30 billion of consumer goods exempt, and an alternative of up to 40% on everything. 22 sectors are named, including robotics and semiconductors.


MOFCOM (商务部) spokesman He Yadong (何亚东) called it unilateralism (单边主义) on Nov 26 and reserved the right to respond, naming no measure. The Hang Seng rose 1.19% the same day. Comments are due Jan 5 and hearings start Jan 25, after the Jan 10 truce expiry.

Key signal: The base case rebuilds today's 20% on a post-IEEPA legal footing; the 40% alternative is the leverage going into the January 10 deadline.
