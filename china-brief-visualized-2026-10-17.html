STORY 1 · AI & TECH
Beijing Drafts Export Controls on 23 AI Technologies, Reaching Model Weights Above 2 Trillion Parameters and Chip Designs at 14nm or Below

MOFCOM and the science ministry published a draft on October 16 adding 40 technologies to China's restricted-export catalogue, 23 of them in AI. Model weights at 2 trillion parameters or a global top-10 ranking, and AI chip designs at 14nm or below, would need clearance to leave China. Comments close November 15, and a temporary registration rule already applies.


APIs, apps and already-public open-source code stay outside the rule. Selling weights to a foreign buyer or running a covered model on foreign servers falls inside it. Kimi K3 and Qwen3.8-Max both clear the reported size bar.

Key signal: Foreign purchases of Chinese labs or their weights now pass through a registration and licensing step, and the draft does not settle whether future open-weight releases above the threshold need approval first.

STORY 2 · AI & TECH
Tim Cook Says Apple Intelligence Is Approved in China on Alibaba's Qwen, With a Test Version Due This Month and No Siri AI

Tim Cook said in Shanghai on October 15 that Apple Intelligence is approved in China, running on Alibaba's Qwen, with a test version later this month and a consumer launch reported for December. Siri AI and Apple's image tools are excluded.


A filter lets regulators approve each model update. Apple's Greater China growth slowed from 38% to 22% over three quarters while local rivals already ship AI features.

Key signal: Apple closes a feature gap about two years late and on regulator-paced updates, and Alibaba gains the largest foreign handset base in China as a Qwen channel on undisclosed terms.

STORY 3 · FINANCE & MACRO
Week Ahead: Q3 GDP Lands Monday With Consensus at 4.4%, a Second Straight Quarter Below the 4.5% Floor of Beijing's Target

NBS releases Q3 GDP and September activity on October 19. The Reuters poll expects 4.4% year-on-year after 4.3% in Q2, with the LPR fixing on October 20 expected to hold.


Q4 needs about 4.3% for the year to average 4.5%, so the annual target is within reach. Retail sales and investment show the weakness, while exports and industrial output carry growth.

Key signal: A 4.4% print keeps the annual target reachable, which lets Beijing keep support targeted, so weak September retail and investment data would shift the debate to the Politburo and December's work conference.
