STORY 1 · AI & TECH / ROBOTICS CAPITAL MARKETS
A P/E North of 200: Unitree Opens Its STAR Market IPO Subscription

Unitree priced its Shanghai STAR Market listing at ¥150.80/share, implying a ~$9B valuation and a price-to-sales ratio above 219x against a roughly 39x industry average. Subscriptions opened today; strategic placement drew China's National Social Security Fund, CNPC, and a 2.31% stake from DeepSeek tied to a joint robotics-AI development pact.


Disclosed H1 2026 financials show growth decelerating sharply (35.6%-45.4% y/y vs 332.6% in H1 2025) even as the offering proceeds — the first public price-discovery event for humanoid robotics as an investable category.

Key signal: The multiple the market assigns here will likely anchor valuation expectations for the entire unlisted humanoid-robotics peer group.

STORY 2 · AI & TECH / FOUNDATION MODELS
Alibaba Prepares to Give Away Its Flagship: Qwen3.8-Max Open Weights Land This Week

Alibaba will publish open weights for Qwen3.8-Max (2.4T total / 95B active parameters) and a smaller Qwen3.8-27B this week — the first time it has open-sourced a Max-class model. Alibaba's self-published benchmarks claim an edge over several rivals on coding and long-context tasks, though none are third-party verified.


License terms remain undisclosed. The strategy accelerates global distribution but sits awkwardly against Alibaba's inclusion in MOFCOM's proposed model-weight export-control consultations.

Key signal: Open-sourcing frontier-scale models is now the norm among top Chinese labs, not the exception — a durable structural shift in how AI capability diffuses globally.

STORY 3 · FINANCE & MACRO / ECONOMIC DATA
Shallow and Slowing: July Inflation Data Undercuts the Reflation Case

July CPI rose 0.5% y/y (core +0.9%) but fell 0.1% m/m. PPI rose 3.5% y/y, decelerating from June's 4.1% and missing the 3.8% consensus, while falling 0.7% m/m. Weak energy prices and soft demand were the main drags.


The print lands just after PBOC's pledge to stay "appropriately loose" through 2H2026, and ahead of a Politburo meeting where markets expect more targeted stimulus. The PPI miss suggests Beijing's anti-involution campaign has not yet produced a durable reflation signal.

Key signal: A sustained PPI reacceleration alongside credit growth is the condition to watch before pricing in a genuine reflation trade.
