AI Transparency Code: Draft to Adoption
AI & TECH · THE JAPAN TIMES, NIPPON.COM · AUGUST 25, 2026

Japan's IP Strategy Headquarters moved its generative-AI "Principle Code" from an August 18 draft to formal cabinet adoption on August 25 — a five-week round trip from consultation close to operative policy. The code is non-binding, but it is now the reference standard rights holders and regulators will cite.

Three obligations anchor it: disclose models/training data/collection methods; tell rights holders if their content was used in training, on request; and respond to user copyright-infringement queries. It applies equally to foreign frontier-model providers serving Japanese users.

Code Timeline: Draft to Adoption
Investor Takeaway
Near-term impact is compliance cost, not a business-model shock. Firms already publishing model cards (NEC, Fujitsu, Hitachi's "Anthropic camp") face less adjustment than those relying on undisclosed scraping. Real teeth depend on how aggressively rights holders use the disclosure-request mechanism in coming quarters.
SIGNAL
Draft-to-adoption in 5 weeks signals the government prioritized speed over further revision.
WATCH
Volume of rights-holder disclosure requests filed against foreign frontier labs over Q4 2026.
RISK
Comply-or-explain carries no statutory penalty — enforcement depends entirely on reputational pressure.
Monaka's Rapidus Roadmap
SEMICONDUCTORS · TOM'S HARDWARE, SERVETHEHOME · AUGUST 24, 2026

Fujitsu's Hot Chips 2026 disclosure detailed a 144-core, chiplet-based Monaka CPU — current dies from TSMC, but a 1.4nm "Monaka-X" successor is explicitly targeted at Rapidus's future Chitose fab, with 2029 mass production.

This lands the same week Rapidus CEO Koike reaffirmed the 2H FY2027 2nm volume timeline to Reuters — giving Rapidus its clearest domestic anchor-customer signal yet, against a backdrop of roughly ¥425bn in cumulative funding without a confirmed volume customer.

Rapidus Node Roadmap vs. Monaka Generations
Investor Takeaway
A named domestic anchor tenant reduces — but does not eliminate — Rapidus's customer-pipeline risk. The arrangement still hinges on Rapidus hitting its 2027 volume target and matching TSMC-grade yield economics. Watch for additional domestic chip-design customers following Fujitsu's lead.
SIGNAL
Koike's Reuters comments and Fujitsu's Hot Chips disclosure landed in the same week — a coordinated confidence signal.
WATCH
Whether Rapidus discloses a second named customer before its 2027 volume-production date.
RISK
Monaka-X is a 2029 target — three years out, with 1.4nm development only beginning within 2026.
The BOJ's Pre-Meeting Speech Calendar
MACRO & BOJ · BLOOMBERG, BANK OF JAPAN · AUGUST 27, 2026

Deputy Governor Himino opened the BOJ's three-speech telegraph today in Saitama — the first of three board appearances (Takata Sept 2, Masu Sept 10) ahead of the September 17–18 policy meeting, echoing the pre-hike signaling pattern used in 2024 and June 2026.

The sequence overlaps a G20 finance leaders' meeting (Aug 31–Sep 1) where Governor Ueda may meet US Treasury Secretary Bessent — whose past remarks on Japanese policy have historically preceded BOJ hikes, per Nomura's Mari Iwashita.

Path to the September 17–18 Policy Meeting
Investor Takeaway
The thesis that matters is what has to hold, not just direction: no dovish surprise across the three speeches, no yen relief before the Sep 18 CPI checkpoint, and no fresh external shock. Treat the next three weeks of speeches as the highest-signal data before the meeting itself.
SIGNAL
Swaps price ~80%+ odds of a September 18 hike to 1.25%, up from ~74% a week earlier.
WATCH
August CPI, due Sep 18 — the final hard data checkpoint before the meeting itself.
RISK
Q2 GDP already missed consensus; a soft Takata or Masu tone could reopen the hold-vs-hike debate.
