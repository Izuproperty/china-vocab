STORY 1 · AI DISTILLATION
151 Million and Counting: Anthropic Names Seven Chinese Labs in Its Largest Documented Distillation Operation

Anthropic's Sept 10 threat-intelligence report itemized what last week's US government advisory described only in general terms. Alibaba's Qwen team: 151M exchanges (May-July), the largest campaign ever documented, via 3,500 accounts extracting chain-of-thought traces. Moonshot: ~300K Kimi requests relayed to Claude over 10 days via 5,380 accounts, then served back to users as Kimi's own answers. DeepSeek: 12.1M exchanges over 14 days. Zhipu: 3.4M via 273 accounts. Xiaomi: 400K via coding tools. SenseTime: purchased transcripts from data vendors rather than live relay.


China's government has already rejected the underlying allegation as "groundless." What's new is the level of detail — exchange counts, account numbers, time windows — that turns a general accusation into claims a reader can evaluate lab by lab.

Key signal: The open question isn't whether distillation occurred across six independently-documented campaigns — it's whether it materially explains the labs' benchmark gains, or is one input among genuine architectural gains. Neither Anthropic nor the named labs have published that counterfactual.

STORY 2 · MEMORY CHIPS
82 Percent: CXMT's Commodity-DRAM Bet Posts the Highest Margin in Global Memory

CXMT's Q2 2026 EBIT margin hit 82%, ahead of SK Hynix (76%) and Samsung's semiconductor unit (70%) — the highest quarterly margin any major memory maker posted in the period. The advantage traces to a gap Korean makers opened themselves: redirecting output toward higher-margin HBM for AI accelerators pulled them back from commodity DRAM, and CXMT filled the shortage, lifting its DRAM share to ~10% from ~4% a year earlier as DDR5 prices rose.


The figure reinforces CXMT's Sept 5 earnings (H1 revenue +874% y/y) — genuine profitability, not just a supercycle echo. It doesn't resolve whether CXMT can produce HBM at volume, the segment Huawei's Ascend line depends on.

Key signal: A margin lead in commodity DRAM funds R&D and capacity, but isn't itself evidence the HBM gap is closing — that remains the metric to watch into CXMT's stated end-2026 HBM target.

STORY 3 · SUMMIT PREP
Eleven Days Out: Beijing Lines Up a Tech, EV, and Aerospace Delegation for Xi's Washington Trip

China is assembling a business delegation of tech, EV, and aerospace executives to accompany Xi to Washington for the Sept 24 summit, benchmarked against the Tesla/Apple/Nvidia/Boeing delegation that joined Trump's May Beijing trip. Separately, Vice Premier He Lifeng and Treasury Secretary Bessent are arranging a pre-summit meeting on AI dialogue and trade-investment consultative bodies, mirroring their May Seoul session.


Sources describe the visit as largely symbolic rather than deal-focused, with low expectations for breakthrough given ongoing AI/semiconductor friction (see Story One). The more concrete near-term item is an extension of the Busan trade truce, described as "almost certain."

Key signal: The real signal is less any single announcement than whether the truce extension and delegation optics hold through Sept 24 without a fresh AI or export-control flare-up in the eleven days between now and then.
