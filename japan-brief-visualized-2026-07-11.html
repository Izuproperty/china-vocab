Timing, not contradiction

The quarter covers March–May; JMTBA's June order surge postdates nearly all of it. Orders lead, recognized revenue lags.

The real read

Order intake and guidance matter more than the headline miss. Motion control proxies semi capex; the robotics order book proxies physical-AI demand.

Divergence test

If Q1 orders inflected while revenue lagged, the JMTBA thesis survives. If orders were flat, the automation complex's equity weakness has fundamentals behind it.

Positioning tell

One sentence repricing three markets means the weak-yen and JGB-selloff trades lean on assumed institutional outflows — and are crowded.

Mechanism check

Ministerial encouragement is not a mandate. GPIF allocations move through formal portfolio reviews, not press conferences.

What confirms it

An MOF policy document or GPIF reference-portfolio signal. Absent that, yen direction reverts to oil and the rate differential after the June BOJ hike to 1%.
