SPECIAL EDITION · FINANCE & POLICY
Pan Gongsheng Opens the PBOC's Standing Lending Facility to Foreign Banks and Says the Overnight Rate Could Replace the 7-Day as the Main Policy Rate

At the Financial Street Forum on October 19, Pan admitted locally incorporated foreign banks to the SLF, let eight offshore RMB clearing banks trade onshore FX for their own account, and set up a liquidity backstop for asset managers hit by redemption-driven bond selling.


The PBOC will study making the overnight repo rate its main policy rate in place of the 7-day reverse repo at 1.4%, let strong borrowers price loans off DR001, and retire benchmark deposit and lending rates frozen since 2015. Wu Qing announced no buying program.

Key signal: Foreign banks get the same central-bank funding line as domestic lenders, which lowers what they must hold in onshore buffers, but most of the package is framed as study or pilot and the implementing notices will set its weight.

STORY 1 · AI & TECH · CHIPS
DeepSeek Plans to Pre-Train on Huawei's Ascend 950DT as Soon as Year-End, With Huawei Engineers in Its Offices Shaping Future Chips

The Information reported October 19 that DeepSeek will start pre-training on Ascend 950DT as soon as end-2026, that Huawei engineers sit in DeepSeek's offices feeding its needs into chip designs, and that DeepSeek gets priority on most 960DT supply in Q1 2027.


In September DeepSeek's 160,000-chip Ascend order was reported as inference-only, with training kept on Nvidia. Epoch AI puts Nvidia's B300 at about seven times the Ascend 950's raw arithmetic, and HBM shortages cap 950DT output.

Key signal: A DeepSeek base model trained on Ascend would make Huawei a training supplier and support DeepSeek's IPO case on compute independence, but the plan is "as soon as," depends on HBM supply, and follows an earlier attempt that went back to Nvidia.

STORY 2 · AI & TECH · MODELS
MiniMax Prices M3.1 Flash at $0.15 per Million Input Tokens, Half Its Flagship's Rate, Two Days After DeepSeek's Update

MiniMax launched M3.1 Flash on October 19: 428B parameters, about 23B active, 1M context, text, image and video input, always-on reasoning. It costs $0.15 in and $0.60 out per million tokens, flat, with open weights due October 23.


Its own table shows 62.2 on Automation-Bench against 50.3 for Claude Opus 5 and 73.7 on DeepSWE, below DeepSeek's claimed 77.0, while trailing Opus 5 by 13.7 points on Terminal Bench 4.0. None of it is independently replicated.

Key signal: Chinese labs are cutting list prices at the low end where developers switch easily, which pressures revenue per token at a listed MiniMax already marked down for cash burn, and always-on reasoning can lift the real cost per task above the list price.

STORY 3 · FINANCE & MACRO
Q3 GDP Grew 4.5% Against a 4.4% Forecast, While Fixed-Asset Investment Is Down 7.2% This Year and Property Investment 20.6%

The NBS reported 4.5% year-on-year growth for Q3 (consensus 4.4%, Q2 4.3%) and 1.2% quarter on quarter. September industrial output rose 5.5% and retail sales 1.1%, both above forecast.


Fixed-asset investment fell 7.2% in nine months, private investment 10.4% and property investment 20.6%. Exports rose 36.3% in September, and the NBS called insufficient domestic demand "prominent."

Key signal: Growth at the 4.5% floor with 4.6% banked lowers the case for broad year-end stimulus, but the quarter rests on exports and chip prices while investment and property keep shrinking.
