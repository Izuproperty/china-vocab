Core Risk
国家情报法
Guójiā Qíngtào Fǎ · National Intelligence Law
Article 7 obligates all Chinese companies and citizens to cooperate with state intelligence — no privacy policy overrides it. Unitree is subject to this law; no independent security audit of its humanoid product line has been published.
Unverified
Whether NVIDIA's Blackwell authentication layer is sufficient to address the National Intelligence Law obligation. Structural legal risk is not a product feature — it is the operative law of Unitree's home jurisdiction, and mitigation claims have not been independently tested.
