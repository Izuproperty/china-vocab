STORY 1 · AI & TECH · ROBOTICS & CAPITAL MARKETS
The Fastest IPO on Record Comes With the Slowest Growth Quarter: Unitree Sets August 10 Subscription

Unitree set its STAR Market subscription for August 10, targeting up to ¥4.2B in proceeds after the fastest CSRC review on record (104 days). But H1 2026 revenue growth is forecast at 35.6%–45.4%, down from 332.64% a year earlier, and adjusted net profit is expected to fall 6.4%–22.0% y/y.


Humanoid ASP has fallen from ¥593,400 (2023) to ¥166,400 (2025) — a volume-over-price strategy that has pulled segment gross margin from 87.67% to 63.18%, even as humanoids became the largest revenue line. Founder Wang Xingxing retains 68.78% voting control on a 23.82% stake via special voting rights.

Key signal: The IPO tests whether volume-over-price economics can hold once growth normalizes — margins are still elevated, but the growth cushion that masked declining unit economics is gone.

STORY 2 · AI & TECH · POLICY & GEOPOLITICS
Cheap and Open Beats Expensive and Closed: Beijing Presses Its AI Advantage Across Asia

At the July 23 APEC Digital and AI Ministerial Meeting in Chengdu, Vice Premier Zhang Guoqing pitched joint AI standards built around open-source cooperation. All 21 APEC economies, including the US, agreed to back open-source AI "with strong security" — a rare consensus point.


The pitch rests on cost: Chinese open-source models run an estimated 60%–90% cheaper than leading US offerings, an argument that lands in emerging Asian economies where compute budgets and "digital sovereignty" are live policy constraints, not abstractions.

Key signal: The more consequential variable is which stack — open Chinese weights or bundled US full-stack offerings — gets embedded into ASEAN and South Asian digital infrastructure over the next 1–2 years, not this meeting itself.

STORY 3 · FINANCE & MACRO · BANKING SECTOR
Big Four Over Everything Else: Foreign Brokerages Position Ahead of China's Bank Earnings Season

BofA projects H1 bank H-share net profit growth improving to 1.7% from 1.1% in Q1, upgrading Agricultural Bank of China and China Merchants Bank while downgrading China Construction Bank A-shares. UBS expects Q2 state-owned bank profit growth of 3.6%, reporting from August 14.


Underneath: loan growth has decelerated from 6.2% (Dec 2025) to 5.2% (Jun 2026), and retail loans contracted as a share of new lending. NIM held roughly flat in Q1–Q2 but faces renewed pressure in 2027 as deposit-repricing benefits fade.

Key signal: This is a rotation into high-dividend state banks over weaker joint-stock names, not a call on sector growth — it assumes capital stays defensive rather than chasing AI-linked names.
