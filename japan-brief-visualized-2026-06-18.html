Insurance / Bank Exposure
Japan's life insurers and regional banks hold large JGB portfolios. As yields rise, unrealized losses accumulate. A rapid drift toward 3% would accelerate mark-to-market pressure ahead of September half-year reporting.
Carry Trade Calculus
The yen held near 160 after the hike because the 180bp US-Japan carry spread cannot be closed by 25bp alone. Himino's forward guidance didn't signal accelerated tightening — carry traders are staying long USD/short JPY.
Next Signpost
Japan May CPI due June 19. If core prints above the BOJ's 2.5-3.0% FY2026 forecast range, pressure on Himino to signal an accelerated rate path will build ahead of the July 30-31 MPM meeting.
