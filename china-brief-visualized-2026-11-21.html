STORY 1 · AI & TECH · CHIPS / CLOUD
Alibaba Says It Is Training Its Next Qwen Models on T-Head Chips, as AI Cloud Revenue Rises 53% and Capex Climbs 60%

Eddie Wu (吴泳铭) said Alibaba has begun training next-generation Qwen (通义千问) models on T-Head (平头哥) chips. The Zhenwu V900 reaches mass production in Q1 2027, and most new AI chip supply after 2027 will be in-house.


Capex (资本开支) rose 60% to ¥50.5 billion ($7.6 billion) and free cash flow (自由现金流) was minus ¥34.5 billion ($5.2 billion). Wu cut next year's cloud growth range to 30-45%, citing US compute (算力) export rules.

Key signal: Alibaba is paying for supply independence with cash burn and slower cloud growth, and its model roadmap now depends on SMIC and domestic HBM output.

STORY 2 · AI & TECH · EXPORT CONTROLS
US Licence Data Show About 880,000 AI Chips Approved for Chinese Buyers Out of 3.4 Million Requested, With Alibaba Cleared for 75,000 of the 950,000 It Sought

The House Foreign Affairs Committee (美国众议院外交委员会) released licensing data on Nov 19. Tencent got 150,000 chips, Alibaba 75,000, Baidu (百度) 43,000 and BYD 4,000, from December 2025 to May 2026.


All four have appeared on the Pentagon's 1260H military-linked company list (涉军企业清单). BIS head Jeffrey Kessler has been asked to testify in early December.

Key signal: Approvals were selective and are now under congressional review, so licensed but undelivered volume is less likely to ship.

STORY 3 · FINANCE · SOVEREIGN DEBT
China's $2 Billion Three-Year Dollar Bond Draws $72.3 Billion of Orders and Prices Flat to Treasuries at 4.821%

The Ministry of Finance (财政部) sold $2 billion of three-year dollar bonds (美元债) in Hong Kong on Nov 19 at 4.821%, with $72.3 billion of orders from 590+ investors.


Sovereign buyers, including sovereign wealth funds (主权财富基金), took 59%. The PBOC held the LPR at 3.0% and 3.5% on Nov 20 for an 18th month.

Key signal: A zero spread to Treasuries gives SOE and LGFV issuers a tight offshore benchmark, though official buyers drove most of the demand.
