STORY 1 · AI / DIPLOMACY South China Morning Post
USCC delegation: seven years, zero tech company meetings
The USCC's first China visit since 2019 covered Beijing, Hangzhou, and Shanghai — all major AI hubs. Every major Chinese AI firm declined. The pattern aligns with MOFCOM's ongoing consultations on model-weight controls and Beijing's broader tech-sovereignty posture under the 15th Five-Year Plan. Engaging with foreign government delegations carries regulatory risk when export-control scrutiny (审查) is active.
Investor read-through: Chinese AI due-diligence channels are narrowing. The premium on local-network access will rise as direct corporate engagement with foreign delegations becomes costly.
