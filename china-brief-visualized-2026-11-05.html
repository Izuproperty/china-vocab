STORY 1 · AI & TECH · FOUNDATION MODELS
Tencent Releases Hunyuan Hy4, a 770-Billion-Parameter Model Priced at ¥18 ($2.70) per Million Output Tokens, and Says It Still Trails the Top Closed Models

Tencent formally released Hy4 on November 4, about ten weeks after the August preview. The series is 770 billion parameters, up from under 300 billion for Hy3, and adds image understanding and computer use for Blender, CAD and cross-app tasks. It already runs in Yuanbao, WorkBuddy, CodeBuddy, WeCom and ima, and is listed on OpenRouter.


The Hunyuan team said a large gap remains with the best closed models (闭源模型). No general benchmark table came with the launch; the one score given was 30.4/100 on Tencent's own biosafety test. Weights and a system card are due "soon", with a cheaper Hy4 Flash to follow.

Key signal: Tencent now has a house model across its own apps and a disclosure-heavy safety pitch that fits the NDRC's planned AI law; without weights or third-party scores, its rank against DeepSeek and Qwen remains unverified.

STORY 2 · AI & TECH · CONSUMER AI
Apple Opens a China Developer Beta of Apple Intelligence on November 4, With Alibaba's Qwen Handling Selected Siri, Writing and Visual Requests

The iOS 27.3 developer beta released on November 4 brings Apple Intelligence to mainland China, with a public release due in December. Qwen (通义千问) supports selected functions in Siri, Writing Tools and Visual Intelligence; users sign in with a Qwen account or Sign in with Apple. Support covers iPhone 15 Pro and later in English or Simplified Chinese.


Siri AI and other WWDC 2026 features are excluded, and other devices wait for further approvals. Apple's Greater China revenue rose 21% in the September quarter and 28% in fiscal 2026. Commercial terms with Alibaba are undisclosed.

Key signal: Qwen gains default-assistant distribution on China's top-selling premium phones, but Apple's China feature set stays a step behind domestic rivals until Siri AI clears its own filing.

STORY 3 · FINANCE & MACRO · SERVICES ACTIVITY
China's RatingDog Services PMI Falls to 50.8 in October, a Three-Month Low, as Input Costs Rise at the Fastest Pace in Five Months While Firms Cut Prices

The RatingDog/S&P Global services PMI fell to 50.8 in October from 51.6, its lowest since July and a 22nd month of expansion. The composite slipped to 52.0 from 52.4. New business held its pace, export orders rose for a sixth month, hiring continued and backlogs fell for the first time since October 2025.


Input costs (投入成本) rose at the fastest pace in five months while firms cut selling prices for a second month, and confidence hit a three-month low. The official NBS services index read 50.0 and non-manufacturing 49.7.

Key signal: Service firms are still hiring on steady orders but cannot pass on rising costs, a margin squeeze that weighs on later hiring and keeps core inflation and consumption support on the policy agenda.
