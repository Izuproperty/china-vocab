The Hike (Locked In)
25bp to 1.00% on June 16 is near-certain (49/51 economists, 80–96% OIS). The hike itself is not the decision that matters for positioning — the press conference language is.
The Iran Wrinkle
Trump's Iran peace signals on June 12 sent oil lower. If Iran-U.S. deal materializes, Japan's PPI falls, cost-push inflation eases, and the September hike case weakens significantly. Ueda faces a genuine communication challenge on H2 guidance.
What to Watch June 16
Key phrase: "in accordance with economic, price, and financial developments." If retained verbatim, September probability stays low (10–15%). If weakened or dropped, expect September to reprice toward 35–40%. Watch yen reaction within 30 minutes of press conference start.
