UK-Japan Bilateral Technology Partnership Timeline (Selected Milestones)
AI Safety Interlock
Japan AISI and UK AISI to coordinate on AI evaluation methods through the International Network for Advanced AI Measurement. Both nations committed to aligning on AI export control standards.
Semiconductor Corridor
Partnership commits both nations to "interlocking AI semiconductor strengths." Formal Rapidus-BSC production agreement creates the first hardware pillar of this corridor. Italy cooperation adds a third node.
Governance Positioning
Japan has now anchored to three AI governance coalitions: G7 Hiroshima AI Process, U.S. Genesis Mission, and UK Frontier Tech Partnership. The 2027 Japan-hosted AI Summit would consolidate this influence.

First international production partnership. British Semiconductor Centre gives UK fabless firms a pathway to Rapidus 2nm manufacturing — alternative to TSMC or Samsung for next-gen chips.
Italian Research Cooperation
Japan Times reported June 11 that Rapidus also signed cooperation agreements with Italian research institutions. Scope undisclosed — likely quantum photonics, materials science, or specialty chemical processes.
Key Risk: Yield Disclosure

BOJ Policy Rate Path: 2024–2026 (Actual + Expected)
Ueda Absent
Gov. Ueda (植田和男) hospitalized June 10 for liver cyst infection. First governor absence at a BOJ scheduled meeting since 1998. Deputy Gov. Uchida (内田眞一) chairs. Institutional decision expected to hold; comms risk elevated.
Carry Trade Watch
Uchida's August 2024 dovish pivot triggered the yen carry trade unwind and the largest single-day Nikkei decline since 1987. His language at today's press conference determines whether the rate path beyond 1.00% is telegraphed or deferred.
Next Data Point
July 30–31 BOJ meeting is when Ueda is expected to return. At 1.00%, the pace of further normalization is the central question. Markets currently imply roughly two additional hikes by end-2026, implying 1.50% by December.
