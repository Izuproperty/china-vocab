Officiating Moat
Hawk-Eye's high switching costs — multi-year stadium sensor retrofits, proprietary data formats, and certified referee training — make Sony's position durable in football, rugby, cricket, and tennis through the 2030 World Cup cycle.
Data Asset Angle
Biomechanical records on 736 professional athletes in live competitive conditions have licensing value to clubs, recruitment platforms, and sports insurance actuaries. This dataset does not exist elsewhere at scale.
Risk to Watch
If a high-profile officiating error occurs at the World Cup, the reputational damage would fall on Sony's Hawk-Eye brand. The 2026 tournament is both the greatest marketing opportunity and the greatest public exposure risk in the system's history.

PSMC Supply Chain Signal
PSMC's co-presentation indicates fabrication involvement — likely interposers or test DRAM. If confirmed, this provides a volume production pathway independent of Japan-only capacity, de-risking the commercialization timeline.
Rapidus Timeline Overlap
HB3DM targets commercialization in fiscal 2029 — two years after Rapidus's 2nm production start. The possibility of pairing Rapidus logic with SAIMEMORY-derived memory in a domestic AI chip is speculative but not beyond the horizon of Japan's METI roadmap.
Conference Risk
VLSI peer review is rigorous. If the via-in-one TSV architecture has manufacturability gaps — substrate yield, thermal management, or via resistance — that will surface in the Q&A sessions. Watch for skepticism from TSMC or Samsung respondents.

Ueda Absent — Uchida Chairs
Governor Ueda's hospitalization means Deputy Governor Uchida conducts the press conference. Uchida is historically more guarded than Ueda about projecting policy trajectories. Any non-committal tone on the September-to-December path may temporarily relieve yen pressure.
Real Rate Still Negative
A policy rate of 1.00% against May PPI of +6.3% and core CPI persistently above 2% implies a deeply negative real rate. The June hike is normalization in name only; the more substantive question is the pace of the next 25-50bp from H2 2026 onward.
Yen and Equity Divergence
The Nikkei 225 rose 2.9% on Friday, partly driven by oil price relief. A rate hike should in theory support the yen, but USD/JPY at 160 reflects that the market expects the hike and is pricing further US-Japan rate differential compression very slowly. Watch Monday's post-decision yen move as a signal.
