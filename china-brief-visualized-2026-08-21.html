STORY 1 · AI & TECH / EARNINGS
76% vs. 9%: Alibaba's Profit Falls Faster Than Its Capex Can Justify

Alibaba's fiscal Q1 2026 net income fell 76% y/y to ¥10.54B against 9% revenue growth to ¥268.95B, as AI capex hit ¥67.68B (+75% y/y) — running ahead of the pace implied by its own 3-year ¥380B AI-infra commitment. Qwen crossed 300M MAU.


The spread between profit decline and revenue growth is wide enough that a user-count metric alone can't underwrite it. The next 1-2 quarters need to show cloud margin stabilization, not just top-line growth, before this reads as a one-off rather than the new baseline.

Key signal: Watch Alibaba Cloud's own margin line next quarter — not Qwen MAU — for evidence the capex is monetizing.

STORY 2 · AI & TECH / EARNINGS
42.6% Down, 28.2% Up: Xiaomi's Chip Bill Meets Its EV Boom

Xiaomi's Q2 net profit fell 20.3% y/y to ¥9.5B (adjusted: -42.6% to ¥6.2B) as DRAM costs compressed smartphone gross margin to 8.5%, despite record ¥1,351 ASP. Smart EV deliveries rose 28.2% y/y to 104,199 units; SU7 cumulative deliveries passed 500,000.


The in-house Xuanjie O1 chip (1M+ units shipped) hedges processor cost but not the DRAM cost that hit this quarter. The same memory supercycle lifting CXMT and SMIC is squeezing the device makers who buy that memory — a bifurcation to watch across this quarter's China hardware earnings.

Key signal: Memory-chip inflation is now a device-maker margin story, not just a chipmaker windfall story.

STORY 3 · MACRO & MARKETS / FISCAL POLICY
¥800 Billion, Redirected: Beijing Tells State Banks to Find Private Borrowers

NDRC Vice Chairman Yue Xiuhu chaired an Aug 14 meeting with China Development Bank, Ex-Im Bank, and the Agricultural Development Bank to accelerate the ¥800B ($118.6B) policy-financial-instrument rollout announced Aug 1, explicitly directing more low-cost, medium-to-long-term funding toward private investment projects.


Naming three policy banks to prioritize private borrowers tacitly concedes that the crowding-in effect from prior stimulus hasn't materialized on its own. The figure to watch isn't the ¥800B total — already known — but whether Q3 private fixed-asset investment data shows the money reaching genuinely private balance sheets.

Key signal: Q3 private fixed-asset investment data is the real test of whether this mechanism works, not the headline figure.
