Core Claim · Primary Risk
Li Qiang's Summer Davos pledge is consistent and directional but does not represent a new policy mechanism. The risk for investors who take the consumption pivot at face value without a specific instrument: the export surge is masking the structural weakness in domestic demand. May retail sales fell YoY for the first time since December 2022, and the
脱钩tuōgōu · decoupling

Unverified · Watch
Whether Beijing will follow Summer Davos with an incremental domestic consumption stimulus instrument — or a specific market-access opening timeline for foreign financial institutions — is unknown. The forum is an external-signaling venue; follow-through via NDRC or State Council circulars in the following two weeks is the measurable test.
Investable Signal
China's record May export run (+19.4% YoY to $376.8B) alongside the WTO's 1.9% global goods-trade growth forecast suggests China is capturing global market share in manufactured goods.
