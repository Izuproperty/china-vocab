What went right

The reopening test came back bid — domestic dip-buyers absorbed the post-rout tape, with the most oversold names (Kioxia) leading. No follow-through selling from the Korea-correlated memory trade.
What it doesn't prove

For the AI/chip complex
積極財政sekkyoku zaisei · proactive fiscal policy
A standing fiscal put: Rapidus, Noetra and the physical-AI programs all sit inside the 17-sector, ¥370tn strategy. Industrial-policy beneficiaries keep their sponsor.
The deleted phrase
財政健全化zaisei kenzenka · fiscal consolidation
Dropped from the document for the first time in years, replaced by a debt-to-GDP "stable reduction" anchor — credible only if nominal growth outruns deficits.
Where doubt shows up
財政規律zaisei kiritsu · fiscal discipline
JGB auctions and the super-long end, with the long rate near 30-year highs, the yen near four-decade lows, and the July 30 MPM the next test of the BOJ-government boundary.
