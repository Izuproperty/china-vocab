STORY 1 · AI & TECH
BYD Registers the Design of Its Own Humanoid Robot, Moving From Investor and Test Customer to Hardware Owner

CNIPA published BYD's design patent CN310247768S for an "embodied intelligent robot" on October 9, after approval on October 6. The drawings closely match Xiao Di, the 1.61 m, 58.5 kg, 31-degree-of-freedom humanoid BYD showed in August.


BYD has backed AgiBot and Percibot and tested UBTech's Walker S1 in its own plant. Owning its design makes it a competitor to the makers it bought from, with in-house batteries, motors and electronics to keep costs down.

Key signal: BYD now owns the design of its humanoid after years as an investor and test customer, which adds a low-cost, vertically integrated competitor to the listed humanoid makers it once bought from.

STORY 2 · AI & TECH
Beijing Launches an AI Employment Drive as Urban Hiring Reaches 87.7% of the Year's Target

MOHRSS Vice Minister Li Zhong said on October 10 that China will run an AI employment action with AI literacy training for all workers, as one of five actions in a 2026–2030 package. Urban job creation reached 10.52 million in nine months.


No budget came with the AI action. Draft gig-worker rules published October 8, covering pay, hours and bargaining, carry the clearest cost for platform companies.

Key signal: Beijing's answer to AI job losses is training and gig-worker rules with no new money attached, so the draft labour protections for platform workers are the part most likely to raise company costs.

STORY 3 · FINANCE & MACRO
Local Governments Get ¥550 Billion of Unused Debt Quota, More Than Beijing Released in 2024 or 2025

The Ministry of Finance released ¥550 billion of unused local debt quota on October 9: ¥300 billion to counties and districts for general budgets and ¥250 billion in special bonds for projects with Q4 funding needs.


Local debt of ¥60.7 trillion sits below the ¥63.2 trillion ceiling, so no legislative vote was needed. The release uses about 40% of the ~¥1.4 trillion available and equals roughly 0.4% of GDP.

Key signal: Beijing is spending ¥550 billion of borrowing room it already had, which supports fourth-quarter growth without a new deficit decision, though it is small against the economy and steers project money to regions that are already stronger.
