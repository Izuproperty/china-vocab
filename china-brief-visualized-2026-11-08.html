STORY 1 · AI & TECH · CHIPS / COMPUTE SUPPLY
About 600,000 Nvidia H200s Sit in Hong Kong as Beijing Withholds Import Approval, Pushing China's Big AI Buyers Toward Domestic Inference Chips

About 600,000 Nvidia (英伟达) H200s are held in Hong Kong because Beijing has not approved large-scale imports, Reuters reported on November 5. Several big internet firms got about 10,000 each, and total approvals could stay under 200,000. Beijing wants Alibaba, ByteDance, Tencent and DeepSeek to run more inference (推理) on Chinese chips.


An eight-GPU H200 server cost more than ¥6 million ($900,000) in China at end-October, about triple a year earlier; Blackwell servers topped ¥20 million ($3.0 million). Nvidia says H200 China sales are under 1% of data center revenue and took a $400 million inventory charge.

Key signal: Beijing's import approval (进口审批) now rations foreign chips on top of US controls, which supports domestic chip order books and raises compute costs for Chinese labs, while domestic HBM shortages limit how fast Ascend can fill the gap.

STORY 2 · AI & TECH · FOUNDATION MODELS / COMPUTE ACCESS
Moonshot AI Sought European Clusters of 8,000-Plus Nvidia Blackwell GPUs at a 30% to 50% Premium, as Kimi Takes 61% of US Enterprise Open-Model Spending

Moonshot AI (月之暗面) held talks to rent Blackwell capacity from European neocloud (新兴云服务商) operators, CNBC reported November 5, with one source citing clusters of 8,000-plus GPUs and proposals at a 30% to 50% premium (溢价) over non-Chinese customers. No signed lease was reported. US rules do not cover remote access (远程访问), and the bill to close that gap awaits the Senate.


Demand explains the effort: Menlo Ventures found Chinese models took 89% of US enterprise open-source spending, with Kimi at 61%. Microsoft listed Kimi K3.1 Pro on Foundry on November 5.

Key signal: Kimi's US enterprise growth leans on offshore compute that one US bill could cut off, and the premium Moonshot is willing to pay squeezes inference margins ahead of a Hong Kong listing reported at up to $5 billion.

STORY 3 · FINANCE & MACRO · RESERVES / GOLD
The PBOC Adds 830,000 Ounces of Gold in October, the Largest Purchase of Its 24-Month Streak, as FX Reserves Edge Up to $3.405 Trillion

The PBOC (中国人民银行) lifted gold reserves (黄金储备) by 830,000 ounces to 78.3 million in October, its 24th straight monthly purchase and the largest since November 2024, according to data released November 7. SAFE (国家外汇管理局) put FX reserves (外汇储备) at $3.4052 trillion, up $4.93 billion after September's $38.1 billion drop.


The buying sped up while spot gold fell about 6.8% in October to below $4,000 an ounce. The World Gold Council (世界黄金协会) counted 321 tonnes of net central bank buying in Q3, with the PBOC's roughly 63 tonnes the largest disclosed.

Key signal: The PBOC increased (增持) its gold purchases into a price fall, which gives gold a steady official buyer on dips; at about $310 billion against $3.4 trillion of FX, the shift away from dollar assets stays gradual.
