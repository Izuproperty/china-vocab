STORY 1 · AI & TECH
StepFun Opens the Weights of Its 600-Billion-Parameter Step 5 Preview, With Day-0 Support on Huawei Ascend, MetaX, Biren and Moore Threads

StepFun released BF16 and FP8 weights for Step 5 Preview on October 15: 600B total parameters, 27B active per token, a 1M-token context and text plus image input. Huawei Ascend, MetaX, Biren and Moore Threads all announced day-0 support that night.


OpenRouter ranked it No. 2 on usage as of October 15. StepFun claims $0.71 per task against $5.86 for Claude Opus 5, a figure no one has independently checked. At 600B it falls under the 2T bar in Beijing's draft export controls.

Key signal: Domestic chipmakers gain a frontier-class open model to sell against Nvidia, but no vendor has published per-chip throughput, and HBM supply still caps how many 600GB deployments China's clusters can host.

STORY 2 · AI & TECH · ROBOTICS
China's Telecom Research Academy Says Embodied-AI Bubble Risk Is Building and Predicts a First Shake-Out Within Two to Three Years

CAICT's October 16 report counts 322 embodied-AI deals worth about ¥93.5B in H1 2026, roughly five times a year earlier. The top 20 firms took 59%, and 20 companies now carry ¥10B-plus valuations.


It says deployment lags shipments, most firms fine-tune open robot models, and their base models trail the leaders by about two generations. It expects a first shake-out within two to three years.

Key signal: A state research body now uses bubble language about embodied AI, and with 59% of funding at the top 20, startups outside that group carry most of the consolidation risk.

STORY 3 · FINANCE & MARKETS
China's STAR 50 Has Lost About 40% Since June as the AI Hardware Trade Unwinds, and Regulators Call on the National Team

The STAR 50 is down nearly 40% from its June 30 peak and ChiNext 36%. MetaX, Moore Threads and Unitree are each about half off their highs, and the CSI 300 lost 3.4% on the week. Foreign Stock Connect outflows hit about ¥90B in Q3.


Regulators met on Thursday and urged the national team to buy faster, and ten-plus AI supply-chain firms announced buybacks. MetaX and Hygon guided strong nine-month numbers, but the STAR 50 still trades above 40x forward earnings.

Key signal: State buying has steadied prices without bringing back foreign money, so the equity window that funds DeepSeek, AgiBot and other planned AI listings is narrower than it was in June.
