Dashed marker denotes pending decision. Sources: Cabinet Office draft (June 19, 2026), public reporting.
Why a rewrite in six months
The December plan was promotion-first. Escalating frontier-model cybersecurity risk forced a revision that layers evaluation on top of promotion rather than replacing it.
The institutional tell
The draft positions the Japan AI Safety Institute to run cybersecurity evaluations of frontier models. Whether those evaluations gate procurement is the substantive question.
Investor read
Gating evaluations would favor large domestic integrators who can carry compliance cost; hortatory language would make the revision signaling, not regulation.

What parity pricing says
A challenger declining to discount is selling supply-chain resilience, not cost — and signaling its backers are not asking it to buy market share.
Where the risk sits
At parity, every point of yield shortfall against TSMC comes out of margin — or in practice out of public funds. The strategy lives or dies on budomari.
What to watch
Pilot-line yield disclosures and an anchor customer tape-out committed at parity pricing, not a subsidized teaser, through the autumn.

JMTBA Orders · Year-on-Year Growth
Preliminary figures released July 9, 2026 by the Japan Machine Tool Builders' Association. Orders have risen year-on-year since July 2025.
Against the tape
Tokyo's equipment and automation names sold off this week on capacity doubts; the order data, a leading capex indicator, accelerated instead.
Caveats first
Preliminary data, a soft year-ago base, and lumpy monthly series — one strong print does not by itself refute a positioning unwind.
Confirmation tests
Yaskawa's Q1 print today and TSMC's June revenue within days. If Yaskawa's orders echo JMTBA, the order-flow vs. positioning divergence is the trade to interrogate.
