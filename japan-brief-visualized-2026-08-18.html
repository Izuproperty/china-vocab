STORY ONE · AI & TECH
Nvidia to Invest $1.5 Billion in SoftBank's SB Energy as OpenAI's Ohio Buildout Financing Takes Shape
Nvidia is putting $1.5 billion into SB Energy, SoftBank's US data-center developer, as part of a financing package that also scales back Nvidia's infrastructure-debt guarantee for the Ohio campus from an earlier $250bn ceiling to roughly $105bn. SB Energy is targeting an IPO as soon as next month that could raise at least $5bn.
Nvidia's Ohio Campus Guarantee: Original Ceiling vs. Revised
Investor Takeaway
Watch whether SB Energy's IPO actually prices at a valuation that validates using subsidiary listings to fund AI infrastructure commitments — the guarantee scale-back is a mild signal of caution on the ultimate size of the Ohio build.
SIGNAL
SB Energy IPO targeted as soon as next month, reportedly aiming to raise at least $5bn.
WATCH
Whether Nvidia's guarantee cut to ~$105bn reflects project-specific caution or broader AI capex discipline.
RISK
SoftBank's reliance on subsidiary stake sales/IPOs to fund OpenAI and Stargate commitments concentrates execution risk in capital-markets timing.
STORY TWO · SEMICONDUCTORS
Sony and TSMC Sign Binding Agreement for ¥747 Billion Kumamoto Image-Sensor Joint Venture
Sony Semiconductor Solutions and TSMC converted their August 10 framework into a legally binding joint-venture agreement on August 11 — five months ahead of the originally targeted March 2027 formalization — with Sony holding 62% and TSMC 38% of the ¥747bn venture. Mass production is targeted for 2029.
Advanced Vision Semiconductor Manufacturing Corp. — Ownership Split
Investor Takeaway
Anchor demand from Sony de-risks JASM Kumamoto utilization as later fabs ramp, and signals Sony expects durable demand for vision sensors beyond smartphones — into industrial robotics and physical AI.
SIGNAL
Binding agreement signed a year-plus ahead of the original schedule — both parties want capacity locked in early.
WATCH
Whether the ¥747bn figure (refined from an initially reported ¥1tn) holds as the venture moves toward construction.
RISK
2029 production start leaves a long window for demand assumptions in autos, robotics, and physical AI cameras to shift.
STORY THREE · MACRO & BOJ
Japan's Q2 GDP Undershoots Forecasts, but September BOJ Hike Odds Hold Near 80%
Preliminary Q2 real GDP grew just +0.3% q/q (+1.1% annualized) versus a ~+2.1% annualized consensus, on soft capex and consumption. Markets shrugged off the miss: swaps still price roughly an 80% chance of a September 18 hike, and JGB yields hit a fresh three-decade high on the 2.6% GDP deflator reading.
Q2 GDP: Consensus vs. Actual, and September Hike Odds
Investor Takeaway
The market is pricing this as an inflation story, not a growth story — favor Japanese financials, which benefit from a hike regardless of the softer demand backdrop, over growth-sensitive cyclicals until CPI data confirms which reading dominates.
SIGNAL
GDP deflator +2.6% YoY — inflation reading stayed dominant even as the growth headline missed.
WATCH
July CPI (Aug 21) and August CPI (Sep 18) — the hard checkpoints before the Sept 17-18 BOJ meeting.
RISK
If the GDP miss proves to be more than a one-off, it hands the BOJ's wait-for-more-data camp fresh grounds to push the hike to October.
