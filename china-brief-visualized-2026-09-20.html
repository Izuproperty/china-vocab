STORY 1 · AI & TECH
$670 Million In: Spirit AI's Founder Puts a Mid-2027 Date on the Robot Brain Breakthrough

Spirit AI (灵犀AI), a 300-person Beijing embodied-AI startup founded in 2024, has raised over $670M since inception, with Moz1 wheeled humanoid units already deployed on CATL's battery-assembly line and inside JD.com retail stores. Founder Han Fengtao says a ChatGPT-style "robot brain" breakthrough could land as soon as mid-2027.


Han's own caveat matters as much as the claim: useful in-home deployment is still roughly eight years out, gated by a physical-interaction data bottleneck that funding alone doesn't resolve. The model-capability timeline and the commercial-deployment timeline are separate bets.

Key signal: Spirit AI's shareholder register — CATL, JD.com, Huawei, Xiaomi, TCL — functions as a distribution and data-access deal as much as a financing round, an asset that holds regardless of when the brain breakthrough lands.

STORY 2 · AI & TECH
2,000 Experts, One Scorecard: China's Robot Summit Tries to Standardize Embodied AI

The ninth China Robot Summit convened 2,000+ executives and researchers in Hangzhou, Sept 16-18. The concrete outputs were infrastructure, not product launches: a standardized capability-grading framework for embodied AI, a national healthcare-robotics collaboration platform, and a global-expansion roadmap.


A shared evaluation standard matters because there's currently no consistent way to compare capability claims across Unitree, UBTech and Spirit AI — benchmarks are self-reported, which is part of why bubble concerns persist even as deployment climbs.

Key signal: The healthcare robotics platform is the concrete near-term signal — state-backed coordination channels in China have tended to precede where public procurement capital flows next.

STORY 3 · FINANCE & MACRO
Eight Sessions Running: PBOC Guides the Yuan to a Four-Year High Before Trump Meets Xi

The PBOC strengthened its daily yuan fixing for an eighth consecutive session this week, the longest streak since 2023. The offshore yuan climbed as high as 6.6957/USD on Friday — its strongest since July 2022 — with the official fix held below market levels throughout, signaling guided, gradual appreciation.


The streak lands in the same week as Bessent-He Lifeng preparatory talks ahead of the Sept 24 Trump-Xi summit, and alongside Governor Pan Gongsheng's framing of a structurally slower credit-growth era — both point toward monetary policy staying in a holding pattern while other levers do the work.

Key signal: Whether the fixing streak continues after Sept 24 is the real test — persistence past the summit would mark a genuine shift toward faster appreciation, not just diplomatic signaling.
