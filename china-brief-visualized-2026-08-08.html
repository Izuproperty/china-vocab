STORY 1 · AI & TECH / FUNDING
Three Moves in One Week: DeepSeek Reopens Its $8 Billion Round, Buys Into Unitree, and Raises Prices

DeepSeek restarted its second external funding round at a ~$74B valuation ask (up from ~$55B in June), with Monolith Management reportedly in talks. In the same week it warned of an API price hike and took a 2.31% strategic stake in Unitree Robotics' Shanghai IPO, alongside a pact to co-develop humanoid-robot AI models.


The read-through: a lab built on training-cost efficiency is now raising capital for its own data centers, charging more, and buying into physical hardware — the same monetization/vertical-integration path Alibaba and Moonshot have already taken.

Key signal: Watch the final round terms — price, structure, and voting rights — before treating the $74B headline valuation as settled.

STORY 2 · AI & TECH / CHIPS
The Earnings Behind the Substitution Story: China's AI Chipmakers Post Triple-Digit Growth

Cambricon's H1 2026 revenue more than doubled y/y to ¥5.99B ($887M); Iluvatar CoreX sales are expected to roughly triple; Moore Threads guided for growth up to 149%. All three sit on Beijing's Anke-certified state AI procurement list.


None disclosed profitability alongside the growth figures — the data confirms adoption of domestic accelerators is accelerating, but says nothing about unit economics, and several of these names already trade at rich multiples relative to their earnings base.

Key signal: Distinguish genuine commercial demand (performance catching up to restricted Nvidia chips) from state-directed procurement pull-forward — the two carry different durability.

STORY 3 · FINANCE & MACRO / TRADE
Above 30 Trillion Yuan: Customs Data Show China's Trade Still Running Hot on Tech Exports

GACC data show 7-month goods trade of ¥30.13 trillion ($4.46 trillion), +17.3% y/y. Exports rose 14%; imports rose 22%, outpacing exports by ~8 points. Mechanical and electrical products made up 63.8% of exports; EV, battery and wind-turbine exports grew 71.2%, 35.8% and 34.8% respectively. July high-tech exports surged over 50%.


Import growth outpacing exports cuts against a narrative of pure export-led growth, though a single release can't separate base effects and front-loaded capital-goods buying from a genuine domestic-demand pickup.

Key signal: Semiconductor and high-tech export strength corroborates the domestic tech-manufacturing re-rating thesis — but durability is unresolved given unsettled US tariff dynamics.
