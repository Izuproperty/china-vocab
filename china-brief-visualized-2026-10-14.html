STORY 1 · AI & TECH
IDC Puts Nvidia's China Share at 34% and Huawei's at 28% for the First Half, Weeks After Huawei Said Ascend Had Pulled Ahead

IDC counts 2.008 million AI accelerators shipped into China in the first half. Nvidia's 678,000 units gave it 34%, and Huawei's 572,000 gave it 28%, with T-Head, Cambricon, Kunlunxin and Hygon adding 19% more.


GPUs fell 26% under export and import limits while other accelerators rose about 80%. Total units grew only about 5%, so IDC's 19% full-year forecast needs a stronger second half.

Key signal: Domestic chips took about half of China's AI accelerator units in the first half, but Nvidia still leads, and Huawei's catch-up depends on HBM supply and on how much compute each chip delivers.

STORY 2 · AI & TECH
StepFun Prices Its STEPX Neo Agent Phone at ¥3,999 With a Year of Model Tokens Bundled In

StepFun's device arm opened pre-orders on October 13 for a ¥3,999 bundle: phone, Step AOS, Step Studio and a 12-month token plan reported at 10 billion tokens a month.


Nearly 100 services, including Alipay, Tencent and Meituan, have signed on. No shipment target, battery size, benchmarks or exact chip were given.

Key signal: StepFun is selling model access as a hardware bundle, and the October 16 first sale and partner integration will show whether an AI lab can carry phone inventory ahead of its IPO.

STORY 3 · TRADE & MACRO
Beijing Says the EU Hybrid Car Deal Lets Exports Grow From 2025, Brussels Says It Halves Them, and EU Leaders Decide on October 15

The EU will cap Chinese hybrid imports at 350,000 a year, rising 6% annually for four years. MOFCOM says that grows exports from 2025's 302,000; Brussels says it halves the 800,000 expected this year.


Hybrids avoided the EU's duties on battery-electric cars and tripled to €6.7 billion in the first half. EU leaders review the deal on October 15.

Key signal: The hybrid cap ends the duty gap that carried Chinese exports into Europe this year, pushing carmakers toward European plants while home retail sales fall 28%.
