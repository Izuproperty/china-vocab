STORY 1 · AI & TECH · ROBOTICS / HUMANOIDS
SAG Counts 16,500 Humanoids Shipped Worldwide in Q3, With AgiBot First at 30% and UBTech Passing Unitree for Second as Full-Size Robots Gain Share

SAG estimates global humanoid shipments of 16,500 in Q3. AgiBot (智元机器人) shipped 4,900, UBTech (优必选) 4,500 and Unitree (宇树科技) about 4,400, with Unitree's share falling to 27% from 37% a year earlier. Chinese vendors made 97% of shipments.


Full-size bipeds (全尺寸双足) rose to 47% of units from 38%, and professional uses reached about 65%. AgiBot and UBTech sell full-size robots to factories and sites; Unitree's base is university labs, and research and education (科研教育) took 31% of units.

Key signal: Growth has moved to full-size industrial robots, which favours AgiBot and UBTech over Unitree; these are analyst estimates, and US vendors start ramping in Q4.

STORY 2 · AI & TECH · FOUNDATION MODELS / CHIPS
Meituan Open-Sources LongCat-2.5, a 1.6-Trillion-Parameter Agent Model It Says Was Trained Entirely on Domestic Chips, Under an MIT License

Meituan (美团) released LongCat-2.5 weights on November 9: a multimodal mixture-of-experts model (混合专家模型) with 1.6 trillion parameters, about 48 billion active, trained on domestic chips (国产芯片) according to Meituan. A 4-bit quantized (量化) version runs INT4 on domestic accelerators.


In-house scores put it level with Claude Opus 5 on BrowseComp and Terminal Bench 2.1, but well behind on Terminal Bench 4.0 and NL2Repo. It follows LongCat-2.0 by four months, also trained on non-Nvidia hardware.

Key signal: A second trillion-parameter model in four months on domestic chips supports Beijing's stance on H200 imports, but the chip, device count and cost are undisclosed and most scores are self-reported.

STORY 3 · FINANCE & MACRO · TRADE
October Exports Rise 35.2% and Imports 41.6% on Chips, Pushing China's 10-Month Surplus Past $1 Trillion as the Monthly Gap Falls Short of Forecasts

Customs (海关总署) said October exports rose 35.2% to $412.3 billion and imports 41.6% to $307.1 billion, both above forecasts. The monthly surplus (贸易顺差) fell to $105.3 billion against a $125.8 billion estimate, and the ten-month total reached $1.04 trillion.


Integrated circuit (集成电路) exports rose 137.1% to $39.6 billion. Imports were driven by chips, gold and crude oil (原油) at its highest since the Middle East war began, which narrowed the surplus without signalling stronger household demand.

Key signal: Chips now drive both exports and imports, so a slowdown in global data-centre spending would hit both sides at once; the $1 trillion surplus lands a week before the expected Xi-Trump meeting at APEC in Shenzhen.
