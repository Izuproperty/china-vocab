SPECIAL EDITION · SUMMIT WRAP
Summit Closes With an AI Incident Channel and a $30 Billion Tariff Carve-Out. Chip Controls Stay Where They Were.

Xi Jinping's three-day Washington visit ended September 26 with a confirmed AI incident channel, reciprocal tariff cuts on about $30 billion of goods each way, and a Chinese commitment to import 10 million tonnes of US coal in 2027–28. US officials said export controls were not on the table.


The trade truce now runs to January 10, 2027, and the next two leader meetings are APEC in Shenzhen in November and the G20 in Miami in December. Those dates, not this summit, are where compute access could move.

Key signal: Tail risk is lower through January, but the chip regime that shapes Chinese AI capex is untouched.

STORY 1 · AI & TECH
Chinese Models Now Handle Most Developer Traffic on OpenRouter, and Two House Committees Want to Know Why

Chinese models took 57–67% of OpenRouter tokens in the week of September 14, up from 6–13% in February. On Vercel the share hit 55% in August from 11% in January. DeepSeek, Z.ai and Alibaba's Qwen lead, and platform executives cite price as the deciding factor.


Volume at this scale lets Chinese labs raise prices without losing share, which supports IPO valuations. Two House committee inquiries into export-control gaps, remote chip access and distillation are the main threat to that trajectory.

Key signal: Chinese labs have pricing power abroad now; the risk has shifted from demand to US regulatory access.

STORY 2 · AI & TECH
Zhipu Strips Repository Uploads From ZCode After Users Found Their Code in a Cloud Bucket

ZCode 3.14.0 removes RepoWiki and the snapshot-upload paths. CAICT found the Alibaba Cloud OSS bucket empty and NSFOCUS confirmed deletion. The client is now open source under Apache-2.0.


No full audit or access logs have been published. Coding agents drive Chinese model adoption abroad, so a data-handling incident in exactly that category gives enterprise buyers a concrete reason to slow procurement.

Key signal: Data handling in coding agents is becoming a procurement gate for Chinese models, on top of the CAC probes already open.
How Zhipu Responded
SIGNAL · FAST FIX
开源kāiyuán · open source
Open-sourcing the client lets outside developers verify the upload paths are gone, which is a stronger remedy than a statement.
WATCH · REGULATOR
网信办Wǎngxìnbàn · CAC
With DeepSeek and Moonshot already under review, Zhipu is a natural next candidate if the CAC widens its sweep.
RISK · ENTERPRISE TRUST
数据安全shùjù ānquán · data security
Without logs, customers cannot confirm what left their machines. That uncertainty slows enterprise deals abroad.
STORY 3 · FINANCE & MACRO
Consumer Stocks Sit Near Ten-Year Lows Going Into Golden Week, With PMIs Due Tuesday

MSCI China consumer sub-indexes are down about 18% in six months. Staples missed profit forecasts by nearly 50% and discretionary by about 10%. August retail sales grew 0.4%.


Industrial profits land Monday and September PMIs on Tuesday, then mainland markets close October 1–7. A firm PMI and strong holiday spending could narrow the valuation gap when trading resumes October 8.

Key signal: The consumer discount is earnings-driven, so it needs a demand catalyst, not just a cheap multiple, to close.
