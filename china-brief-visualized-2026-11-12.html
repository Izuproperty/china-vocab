STORY 1 · AI & TECH · ROBOTICS / EMBODIED AI
AgiBot Open-Sources LIFE, a Lifelong-Memory Framework That Keeps Robots Watching Between Tasks, With RoboBeats Models From 0.8B to 4B Parameters

AgiBot (智元机器人) open-sourced LIFE on November 11: RoboBeats decides when a robot should react or remember, Plastic Memory stores frames and structured records, and Explore–Distill–Mount turns solved problems into reusable skills (能力复用). Weights ship at 0.8B, 2B and 4B parameters.


AgiBot reports FindingDory success rising from 37.5% to 65.8% and a 24-hour test on the A3 Ultra answered in 80.6 seconds versus 554.7 for a Codex baseline. All results are self-reported, and the 24-hour memory was built offline from recorded video.

Key signal: AgiBot is moving its competition with UBTech and Unitree onto software that makes installed robots more useful, but the memory gains are self-reported and were measured on recorded video, not live multi-day deployments.

STORY 2 · AI & TECH · CHIPS / EXPORT CONTROLS
Blacklisted Northwestern Polytechnical University Bought Two Nvidia H200s for ¥874,700 ($131,000) Through a Local Reseller, One of 26 Records Reuters Found of Restricted Chips Reaching Chinese Institutions

Northwestern Polytechnical University (西北工业大学), on the Entity List (实体清单) since 2001, bought two H200s for ¥874,700 ($131,000) from local reseller Shaanxi Bosuan in October, and rents eight-H200 servers for training.


Reuters found 26 records of restricted Nvidia chips reaching Chinese universities and public bodies in 2026. The volumes are small, but the report lands as Congress drafts chip-tracking bills and seeks a briefing from Jensen Huang.

Key signal: The leak is small in units but arrives as Congress drafts chip-tracking and rental rules, so the near-term risk is tighter US controls that cut Nvidia's residual China sales and lift demand for Huawei Ascend.

STORY 3 · FINANCE & MACRO · CONSUMPTION / SUBSIDIES
Double 11 Online Sales Grow 4.0% to ¥1.14 Trillion ($171 Billion) in the Pre-Period as Trade-In Categories Slow to 3.4% and Services Rise 12.1%

Inspur Zhuoshu (浪潮卓数) put Double 11 (双十一) pre-period online sales at ¥1.136 trillion ($171 billion), up 4.0%. Physical goods rose 2.5% and non-physical online retail (非实物网络零售) 12.1%.


Categories on the national trade-in (以旧换新) list grew just 3.4% combined, even as gaming laptops, smartwatches and smart glasses grew fast, so larger appliances lagged. Tmall listed LLM tokens (词元) as a category for the first time.

Key signal: Trade-in subsidies are losing their pull on goods spending while services keep growing, which points next year's consumption support toward services and keeps pressure on appliance and electronics makers that relied on the subsidy boost.
