AI-Native RAN: Trial Results
AI & TECH · SOFTBANK CORP, ERICSSON · AUGUST 20, 2026

SoftBank and Ericsson ran Japan's first live-network validation of an AI-native scheduler for link adaptation — machine-learning decisions injected directly into commercial 5G baseband equipment in real time, rather than static rules-based scheduling.

Results varied by site, but the top-line gains were real: up to ~25% higher spectral efficiency and up to ~50% higher downlink user throughput in the best-performing locations, with an average uplift of roughly 10% across all sites tested.

Performance Gain vs. Conventional Scheduling
Investor Takeaway
This is concrete performance data from a live commercial network, not a pilot announcement. The open question is monetization — whether SoftBank/Ericsson license the approach to other carriers or keep it proprietary as a SoftBank network-quality differentiator ahead of 5G Advanced/6G.
SIGNAL
SoftBank building AI directly into network infrastructure it owns, distinct from its equity-stake AI bets.
WATCH
Whether the AI-native scheduler moves from trial to commercial deployment, and on what timeline.
RISK
Gains were site-dependent — average uplift (~10%) is far more modest than headline best-case figures.
Renesas's Physical AI Footprint: Japan vs. China
SEMICONDUCTORS & ROBOTICS · RENESAS ELECTRONICS, ROBOTICSTOMORROW · AUGUST 27, 2026

Renesas — Japan's largest chipmaker and the world's #1 automotive MCU maker — opened a physical AI and robotics lab in Beijing on August 27, a customer-facing hub for demonstrating and co-developing robotic systems on Renesas silicon, from proof-of-concept through deployment at scale.

The choice of location stands out against Renesas's parallel, Japan-based NEDO tactile-data project with Fanuc, Yaskawa, and Kawasaki Heavy Industries — a reminder that Renesas is defending its embedded-AI franchise on two fronts at once, in markets with very different competitive and geopolitical risk profiles.

Renesas Physical AI Initiatives, 2026
Investor Takeaway
A dual-market hedge: Renesas defends its MCU franchise at home even as domestic rivals in China (D-Robotics, Black Sesame) encroach on automotive silicon — and chooses to engage China's robotics volume market directly rather than cede it.
SIGNAL
Renesas treats China as a volume robotics market worth a dedicated facility, despite competitive risk there.
WATCH
Whether Beijing-lab customer data feeds back into Renesas's core embedded-AI roadmap or stays siloed.
RISK
Widening US/Japan export-control scope could make the dual-market posture harder to sustain over time.
The Nikkei's One-Day Round Trip
MARKETS · NIKKEI, REUTERS, MARKETSCREENER · AUGUST 27, 2026

Nvidia's fiscal Q2 results, released after the US close on August 26, beat expectations across the board — revenue more than doubled YoY to $96.22bn, with data-center revenue up 117% YoY to a record $89bn. Tokyo's chip-linked shares opened sharply higher: Nikkei 225 up more than 500 points at the open, Kioxia up as much as 6%.

By the close, all of it had reversed. The Nikkei finished at 66,131.98, down about 130 points on the day, with Advantest and SoftBank Group both in the red — even as the broader TOPIX extended a sixth straight session of gains.

Nikkei 225, August 27: Open vs. Close
Investor Takeaway
The intraday fade is more informative than the earnings beat: Japan's AI-supply-chain names may be priced closely enough to perfection that even a genuine, broad-based Nvidia beat generates profit-taking rather than sustained follow-through.
SIGNAL
TOPIX extended a sixth straight gaining session even as the Nikkei's AI-linked names faded — a breadth vs. leadership split.
WATCH
Next earnings prints from Tokyo Electron, Advantest, and Kioxia for whether the fade repeats.
RISK
A market this quick to fade good news has limited patience for a genuine disappointment, into the BOJ's Sept. decision.
