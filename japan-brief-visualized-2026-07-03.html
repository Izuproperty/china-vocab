VTLA Dataset — Four Integrated Modalities
Vision · Tactile · Language · Action · combined into one physical-AI training substrate
The Bottleneck
Embodied AI's constraint is not compute but data: force and contact signals are rarely captured and are fragmented across incompatible machines. A shared, tactile-inclusive dataset targets exactly that gap.
Why Cooperation Is Notable
Kawasaki, Fanuc and Yaskawa compete directly in arms and controllers and guard motion data closely. Standardizing specs across three rivals is the unusual part — and the part most likely to fray under commercial pressure.
Where It Sits
One layer below the Noetra national model: Noetra targets the model, this targets the training substrate. Leverage may accrue to whoever controls the dataset and tooling, not any single robot maker.

Rotation, Not Panic
TOPIX held firm as capital moved into banks, software, airlines and trading houses. Positive breadth (1,157 up vs 375 down) on ~¥9.1T turnover is a rotation out of crowded names, not broad de-risking.
Rate Backdrop
A higher risk-free rate makes leveraged growth valuations harder to defend. The chip de-rating and the 2.7% JGB coupon (Tab 3) are two faces of the same repricing.
What Would Confirm Downside
A one-day reset from record levels does not invalidate the AI-capex story. The tell to watch is the order picture — especially memory pricing signals from Kioxia into earnings.

The Soft Tail
The tail widened to 0.2 from 0.05 — thinner demand at these levels. If subsequent long-end auctions stay soft, the market is signaling that supply and a weak yen, not just BOJ policy, set the clearing yield.
Repricing Everything
A 2.7% risk-free 10-year lifts bank net interest margins and raises the discount rate on growth names — the same valuation pressure visible in the Tab 2 chip selloff.
The Counter-Case
Domestic life insurers and banks may step back in as absolute yields reach a generational high. Governor Ueda reiterated readiness to hike further; the next BOJ decision is July 31.
