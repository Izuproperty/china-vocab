China AI Exit Route Availability — Before and After NDRC Technology-Tracing Framework
Core Claim / Primary Risk
The NDRC's 技术溯源jìshù sùyuán · technology tracing approach represents a structural shift in cross-border M&A risk for Chinese AI assets. Any company whose core technology was developed in China is now subject to Chinese jurisdiction regardless of corporate domicile — closing the standard exit route for approximately a decade's worth of offshore-restructured Chinese AI startups.
Unverified / Watch
The scope of "technology origin" is undefined in the new rules — it is unclear where the line sits for companies that developed early versions of their technology in China and subsequently rebuilt or substantially redeveloped it outside China. Enforcement is also untested: the Manus case was the first. The extraterritoriality of the claim (jurisdiction over non-Chinese legal entities based on IP origin) may face pushback in non-Chinese jurisdictions.
Investable Signal
月之暗面Yuèzhī'ànmiàn · Moonshot AI and 阶跃星辰Jiēyuè Xīngchén · StepFun moving toward mainland reincorporation and Hong Kong IPO pathways creates a near-term opportunity to monitor for pre-IPO secondary activity.
