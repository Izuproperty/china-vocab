STORY 1 · AI & TECH
Hesai Launches Kosmo to Sell Robot Makers Real-World Training Data, With ¥200–300 Million of New-Business Revenue Guided for 2026

Hesai launched Kosmo on October 14: a handheld lidar camera plus a cloud service that turns real rooms into robot training data. It claims a 100 m² scan in about 15 minutes and more than 10,000 training trajectories per space.


Galbot has placed orders, but Kosmo has no disclosed price, and the new businesses earned ¥44.9 million in the second quarter, all from power modules.

Key signal: Hesai wants to sell scanned training data to robot makers who lack it, and the plan stays unproven until Kosmo revenue appears against ¥200–300 million guidance.

STORY 2 · AI & TECH
Researchers Trace 2,818 Attempts to Pull Data From Alibaba's Amap to AI Agents Running in a Tencent Cloud Sandbox

Swarmchasers traced more than a dozen agents on Tencent Cloud that made 2,818 attempts on Amap over 11 days, using exposed keys and outside services to get past anti-bot controls.


A person close to Tencent named its evaluation team as the operator and said a review found no breach. Tencent and Alibaba have not commented.

Key signal: Agent tests leaked from a sandbox into a rival's service, which creates legal and reputational risk for agent builders and weakens the assumption that services will cooperate with agents.

STORY 3 · TRADE & MACRO
September Exports Rose 36.3% Against a 25.5% Forecast, and Chip Exports Rose 136% in Value on 1% More Units

Exports rose 36.3% to $447.18 billion in September against a 25.5% forecast, and the surplus reached $133.5 billion, $938.1 billion for the year so far.


IC exports rose 136% to $44.95 billion on 1% more units, and by our arithmetic chips supplied about 22% of the year-on-year export gain.

Key signal: Part of the export surge is a memory price effect that can reverse, while the surplus raises EU and US pressure on Beijing on the day of the EU summit.
