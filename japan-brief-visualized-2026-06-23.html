Japan AI Data Center Investment vs. Opposition Geography (2026)
Regulatory Gap
Japan's Urban Planning Act (都市計画法) permits data centers in quasi-industrial zones shared with residential buildings — no conflict-of-use review required. Inzai City acknowledged this "cannot be adequately addressed within current rules."
Legal Trigger
Inzai 5 project: Colt Technology (London) SPV backed by Mitsui, CPP Investments (Canada), and Fidelity. 52-meter structure adjacent to 15-story condo. Residents document 25% property value loss post-permit. Case now in litigation.
Investor Risk
Site selection risk for Tokyo-metro data center greenfield investment is now demonstrably litigable. No moratorium expected near-term, but METI + MLIT coordination on national zoning standards is a watch item before H2 2026 investment decisions.

Nikkei 225 Trajectory · June 14–22, 2026 (record run context)
Index Structure
Nikkei 225 is price-weighted, not cap-weighted. Tokyo Electron's ~¥36,000 share price gives it ~10% index weight. Every AI fab investment globally flows to TEL's order book and amplifies the Nikkei more than any comparable index.
Rapidus Risk Variable
Government backing approaches ¥3T cumulatively. Pilot line running since Apr 2025. 2nm GAA transistors confirmed Jul 2025. Design-win pipeline beyond Tenstorrent is the execution gap — commercial viability unproven without broader customer commitments.
Rate-Hike Immunity
BOJ raised to 1.0% June 16 — Nikkei hit 70,000 the same day. Structural foreign inflows (~¥16T since Apr 2025) driven by AI infra logic, not carry trade, explains why rate hikes have not disrupted the rally. Watch for disruption if yen breaks below ¥155/$.
