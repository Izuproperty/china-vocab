STORY 1 · CAPITAL MARKETS
$911 Million Raised, 206% Pop: Enflame Closes Out China's "Four Little GPU Dragons" IPO Cycle

Enflame (燧原科技) began trading on Shanghai's STAR Market Sept 11, pricing ~43M new shares at ¥142.18 to raise ~¥6.12B ($911M). Shares closed up 206% — the strongest debut among the "four little GPU dragons" (Enflame, Moore Threads, MetaX, Biren Technology), all now public via the STAR Market's pre-profit "hard tech" exception. 2025 revenue was ~¥990M (up from ¥722M); the company remains unprofitable, with proceeds earmarked for 5th/6th-gen training chips.


The debut-day pop is unremarkable for a thinly floated, policy-favored STAR issuer. What matters more: all four companies carry meaningful customer concentration (Tencent alone was ~84% of Enflame's 2025 revenue) and none has closed the gap to profitability despite fast growth.

Key signal: Watch post-lockup quarters for revenue diversification and loss trajectory — the real test of whether public capital translated into commercial scale.

STORY 2 · CHIP SUPPLY CHAIN
20 to 60 Percent: An HBM Shortage Pushes Huawei and Cambricon to Raise AI Chip Prices

Huawei and Cambricon have raised domestic AI accelerator prices over the past two months as an HBM (high-bandwidth memory) shortage works through China's chip supply chain, per a Reuters exclusive. Huawei's Ascend 950DT (Q4 shipment) is now above ¥250,000 (~$37,000), up 20-50% in two months; the 950PR is up ~30% to above ¥80,000; the older 910C is up from ~¥90,000 to above ¥110,000.


Chinese buyers already pay a multiple of what buyers elsewhere pay for HBM, so this compounds an existing cost disadvantage. Higher per-unit pricing helps near-term chip economics but raises input costs for hyperscalers and labs building compute clusters.

Key signal: HBM output from CXMT and other domestic memory makers — not wafer capacity — is now the binding constraint on China's Nvidia-substitution push.

STORY 3 · PROPERTY & FINANCE
From Announcement to Implementation: Beijing's Property Overhaul Adds a "Lead Bank" Layer

A Sept 8 Xinhua Economic Watch dispatch detailed implementation of the Aug 28 property reform package: presale (预售) phase-out in favor of completed-home sales, supervised buyer-deposit accounts, and a new "lead bank" (牵头银行) system walling off project-level financing to curb cash-shifting between developments. CSRC is separately backing listed-developer M&A.


Pinpoint's Zhang Zhiwei called the package "stronger than expected"; Xinhua cited a Shenzhen pilot project's foot traffic up ~30% post-announcement. The reform is framed as implementing the 15th Five-Year Plan's "new development model" (新发展模式) — a multi-year structural shift, not a one-off stimulus.

Key signal: The real test is whether lead-bank financing reduces cross-project cash commingling at distressed developers — that requires several quarters of completion data, not a single visit count.
