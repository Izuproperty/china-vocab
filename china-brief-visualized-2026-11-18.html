STORY 1 · AI & TECH · FOUNDATION MODELS
Tencent Releases Hy4 flash at $0.07 per Million Input Tokens and Says It Costs One-Twentieth as Much per Task as Its Flagship Hy4

Tencent (腾讯) released Hy4 flash on Nov 17: 224B total and 12B active parameters, a 1M-token context, and list prices of $0.070 per million input tokens and $0.281 per million output tokens.


Tencent's own WorkIndex test puts its cost per task at one-twentieth of Hy4 and one-eighth of DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash. No independent score exists yet, and Baidu's Robin Li (李彦宏) warned the same day that AI could become a bubble without profits.

Key signal: Tencent is pricing agent tasks low enough to subsidise usage across its apps, and the pressure falls on rivals that sell API access for a living.

STORY 2 · AI & TECH · AI GOVERNANCE
Alibaba, Tencent and Moonshot Strip Offensive Cyber Skills From New Models as MOFCOM's Proposed AI Export Ban Moves Toward a Final Rule

Bloomberg reported on Nov 17 that Alibaba, Tencent and Moonshot (月之暗面) have cut offensive cyber abilities from recent models, after MOFCOM (商务部) proposed an export ban (出口禁令) on AI that can attack critical infrastructure.


CrowdStrike linked a DeepSeek model to attacks in 14 countries. MOFCOM's comment period closed Nov 15, so a final rule and its open-source exemption are next.

Key signal: Labs are restricting themselves before MOFCOM finalises its rule; a narrower open-source exemption would shift distribution to hosted APIs.

STORY 3 · FINANCE · MONETARY POLICY
PBOC Drains a Net ¥300 Billion ($45 Billion) Through Outright Reverse Repos in November, Its First Medium-Term Withdrawal Since June

The PBOC (中国人民银行) netted a ¥300 billion ($45 billion) withdrawal (净回笼) through outright reverse repos (买断式逆回购) in November, the first since June, after a ¥400 billion ($60 billion) injection in October.


It came with October data misses and A-shares at a 16-month low. The 10-year yield hit a record intraday low of 1.58% on Nov 6, and the PBOC is moving liquidity (流动性) into overnight operations.

Key signal: Term funding is being drained while data weaken, so bond investors should expect no quick rate cut and watch the month-end MLF.
