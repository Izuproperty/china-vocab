STORY 1 · AI & TECH · FOUNDATION MODELS / CAPITAL
DeepSeek's Registration Filing Names 31 New Backers for a ¥105.5 Billion ($15.8 Billion) Round, With Tencent, CATL, China Mobile and CITIC Securities on Top

A business registration change (工商变更) completed Nov 17 shows a new limited partnership (有限合伙) holding ~17.3% of DeepSeek (深度求索). Registered capital (注册资本) rose 21% to ¥19.92 million ($3.0 million).


Tencent- and CATL-linked funds hold 9.57% of the vehicle each, China Mobile and CITIC Securities (中信证券) 7.65% each. One investor dropped out after its list of underlying investors was not approved.

Key signal: The cap table is all-mainland and screened down to the investors behind each fund, but new capital does not fix the compute shortage that forced DeepSeek to limit service on Nov 15.

STORY 2 · AI & TECH · FOUNDATION MODELS / AGENTS
Former Qwen Lead Lin Junyang's Pragmatik Labs Raises at a $5 Billion Valuation, 2.5 Times Its August Price, With No Product Yet Released

Lin Junyang (林俊旸) left Alibaba's Qwen team in March and launched Pragmatik in August. Bloomberg says a round of tens of millions of dollars on Nov 20 valued it at $5 billion (估值).


New backers are HyT Capital, CDH Investments (鼎晖投资) and Geely (吉利). Gaorong Ventures (高榕创投) and HSG co-led the August round at $2 billion. The company builds AI agents (智能体) for digital and physical settings.

Key signal: Private marks for talent-led labs are still climbing while listed model makers fall, so the price needs a product and paying users in H1 2027.

STORY 3 · FINANCE · EQUITY STRATEGY
Goldman Upgrades Hong Kong-Listed Chinese Stocks to Overweight After a Week That Took the Hang Seng Down 5.2% and the STAR Composite Down 11%

Goldman moved H-shares (H股) to overweight (超配) and A-shares to marketweight (标配) on Nov 21. It sees 15% earnings growth in 2027 and a 23% gain for MSCI China, now at 9.8x forward P/E (市盈率).


On Nov 20 the Hang Seng Tech Index (恒生科技指数) fell 3.8% and MiniMax 10.8% to a record low, after the Nasdaq's worst week in six years.

Key signal: The upgrade is a valuation call within China, and it needs Q4 earnings revisions to turn up before Fed hikes and $58 billion of Q1 lock-up expiries (解禁) weigh.
