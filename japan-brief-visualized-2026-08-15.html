STORY 1 · AI & TECH Nikkei Asia
Silicon Valley's SRI Backs a New Deep-Tech Fund for Japan
Global Innovation Labs — SRI International's venture arm, with lineage running through Siri and Intuitive Surgical — is teaming with Z Venture Capital, LY Corporation's CVC arm, to fund early-stage Japanese startups in AI, space, and defense. ZVC's existing ~$190-193M second fund already expanded past LY's core media/commerce/fintech mandate into deep tech in January 2025, giving the new JV a local capital base and LP network on day one.
Investor read-through: a new, credibly-backed foreign LP channel into Japan's thin early-stage deep-tech market — worth tracking for portfolio-company overlap as the fund deploys, though size and first-close timing remain undisclosed.

STORY 2 · SEMICONDUCTOR & INDUSTRIAL Nikkei Asia / JST
TIER IV Open-Sources Its AI Chip Design for Self-Driving Cars
TIER IV, the Nagoya startup behind the open-source Autoware autonomous-driving stack, joined JST's Next-Generation Edge AI Semiconductor R&D Program to design and open-source the logic, compiler, and toolchain for a Level 4 autonomous-driving inference chip. The bet: repeat Autoware's software playbook in silicon, drawing manufacturers into a shared open platform instead of proprietary, foreign-licensed AI accelerators.
Investor read-through: an R&D-stage signal, not yet a manufacturing one — the thesis only becomes investable once a named chipmaker commits to fabricating the design.

STORY 3 · MACRO & BOJ Reuters
Japan's Bond Curve Reprices as Oil Risk Pushes September Hike Odds to 78%
JGB yields rose across every maturity as oil prices climbed on renewed Strait of Hormuz risk, layering imported-energy inflation on the BOJ's already-hawkish July Summary of Opinions. The 10-year rose to 2.850%, the 5-year hit a record 2.110%, and the 2-year reached its highest since May 1995 — a full-curve move, not just a short-end repricing.
Investor read-through: a broadly higher JGB curve raises the discount rate on Japanese equities near record valuations and tightens the yen carry trade — watch July CPI (Aug 21) and August CPI (Sep 18) as the hard checkpoints before the Board commits to a date.
