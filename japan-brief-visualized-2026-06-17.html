Japan Semiconductor Sector — Key Catalysts: June–September 2026

JASM Phase 2 Significance
The upgrade from 12nm to 3nm changes JASM's strategic category from automotive supply-chain backup to leading-edge logic. Equipment delivery milestones in H2 2026 will signal whether the 2028 mass-production timeline is realistic.
TEL's MATCH Act Exposure
TEL holds near-monopoly share in EUV coater/developer tools. If the MATCH Act triggers FDPR expansion to tools with U.S.-origin components, TEL's China revenue (roughly 25-30% of total) faces acute risk. Management has been publicly quiet — watch for any shift in commentary.
Yen Tailwind/Headwind
BOJ's 1% rate now sets a floor, but the carry remains 180bp wide. If rate hike expectations accelerate toward 1.25% by July, yen strength would compress TEL and Advantest's reported revenue. The currency assumption in July guidance will be pivotal.

BOJ Policy Rate: Actual vs. Societe Generale Projected Path to 2% Terminal

Why Yen Didn't Move
The hike was 100% priced in. Uchida's post-meeting language introduced no hawkish surprise. Without a forward guidance acceleration, carry trade remains profitable at 180bp spread. The yen's "floor" from BOJ hikes requires either explicit rate path guidance OR MoF intervention.
Ueda's Absence Risk
Markets trust Ueda's communication more than Uchida's. The liver cyst hospitalization introduced tail risk: if Ueda's return to July's meeting is uncertain, the BOJ's ability to guide market expectations is impaired precisely as the rate path becomes contested.
Oil Wildcard
US-Iran peace deal talks could reduce Hormuz supply-chain risk and lower oil import costs for Japan. Lower energy inflation would soften the CPI argument for further BOJ tightening — potentially slowing the 25bp-quarterly pace Societe Generale projects.
