STORY 1 · AI & TECH
OpenAI Names Moonshot in a Distillation Campaign: 16,000 Requests in Two Days Aimed at Its Hidden Reasoning

OpenAI said on September 30 that a July campaign to extract its models' encrypted reasoning came from a core cluster of people associated with Moonshot AI. The two-day spike ran to about 16,000 requests from more than 4,000 users, across 15,000+ accounts in total.


Moonshot now faces distillation claims from OpenAI, Anthropic and the White House, plus a CAC data-security probe at home. If Kimi trained partly on US reasoning traces, its cost advantage is smaller than its benchmarks suggest.

Key signal: Two US labs have now documented the case Washington would need for API or export restrictions on Moonshot. Neither step has been taken, and the evidence so far is OpenAI's own account.

STORY 2 · AI & TECH
25,000 Humanoids Shipped in Six Months, and IDC Finds 69% Went to Labs, Classrooms and Showrooms

IDC counted nearly 25,000 humanoid robots shipped worldwide in H1 2026, up 432.1%. AgiBot led with 8,600+ units and Unitree followed with about 5,000; Chinese vendors supplied about 95% of the total.


69% of units went to research, education, exhibition and government data-collection buyers, down from 83.8%. Average prices fell from about $37,000 to under $30,000, so revenue grew more slowly than units.

Key signal: Volume favors China's leaders, but commercial buyers take only about a third of shipments. Unitree's and AgiBot's valuations need that share to keep climbing.

STORY 3 · FINANCE & MACRO
Hong Kong Posts Its Biggest Nine Months of IPOs Since 1980, While Five of September's Seven Debuts Broke Issue

Hong Kong raised US$48.4 billion from 112 main-board IPOs in the first nine months, a record since 1980. Deloitte now forecasts HK$480 billion for the year, above the 2010 record of HK$449.5 billion.


Five of seven September debuts closed below their offer price as the Fed resumed rate increases. Dongshan Precision's US$2-3 billion PCB listing, approved by the CSRC, is the next large test.

Key signal: The pipeline is deep, with 510 applications, but September's broken deals point to wider discounts. Southbound Connect stays shut until mainland markets reopen after October 8.
