STORY 1 · ROBOTICS / IPO
170% Before the Bell: Gray-Market Bids Are Already Pricing Unitree's Debut Pop

Unitree priced its STAR Market IPO at ¥150.80/share (~$9B valuation), with the retail tranche oversubscribed ~8,289x. Ahead of a confirmed trading date, scalpers are offering ¥410/share (+170%) and Hyperliquid perpetuals imply up to 4x upside — all before the stock has traded a single day.


That pricing sits atop a valuation already at ~219x trailing earnings, well above the ~35x sector average, on a company whose own prospectus concedes current humanoids can't yet reliably perform complex real-world tasks.

Key signal: Watch the gap between speculative pre-listing pricing and disclosed product limitations once trading actually opens — not the headline pop.

STORY 2 · AI / CAPITAL CONTROLS
51.8% and Holding: Moonshot Brings In the "National Team" to Clear Its Hong Kong Listing

Moonshot AI converted its domestic entity to a joint-stock company and began dismantling its red-chip structure, drawing in the National AI Industry Investment Fund, the National Social Security Fund, Shanghai/Guizhou guidance funds, and a People's Daily-linked vehicle. Yang Zhilin retains 51.8% control.


This operationalizes April's NDRC directive requiring Moonshot, ByteDance, and StepFun to reject US-origin capital without clearance — state capital in the pre-IPO cap table looks set to become standard for the HK listing pipeline, not a one-off.

Key signal: Regulatory clearance for HK listings increasingly requires backfilling foreign-held structures with state-linked capital — a governance variable international investors haven't had to price before.

STORY 3 · MACRO / DATA RELEASE
3.7%, 5.2%, -12%: China's July Data Confirms the Slowdown Wasn't a One-Quarter Blip

July activity data released Aug 17 showed industrial output at 5.7% y/y (vs. ~6% consensus), retail sales at 3.7% y/y (2026's weakest print), private FAI down 1.5% y/y ytd (steepest since 2020), and unemployment up to 5.2%.


Combined with the sub-50 manufacturing PMI and record-low construction PMI already reported this month, this is the third straight data point confirming a broad-based — not single-indicator — slowdown, raising the bar for what "incremental" H2 stimulus needs to deliver.

Key signal: Three consecutive soft releases across production, consumption, investment, and employment leave less room to dismiss any one print as noise.
