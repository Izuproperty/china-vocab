STORY 1 · AI & TECH / FOUNDATION MODELS
320 Billion Params, No Name Tag: Zhipu's Stealth Model Outs Itself as GLM-5.3-Flash

Zhipu AI (智谱AI) confirmed Aug 26 that "Ox Alpha," an unbranded model quietly running on OpenCode and OpenRouter, was GLM-5.3-Flash — a 320B-parameter MoE (18B active/token), natively multimodal, with a 1,048,576-token context window. Anonymous testing let Zhipu gather adoption signal before the branded reveal; the model scored 57 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index at $0.045/task, a cost level AA says was previously ~10x higher.


The benchmark numbers are Zhipu/AA-sourced, not independently reproduced — the same caveat attached to nearly every Chinese frontier release this quarter. The more durable signal is the go-to-market mechanic itself: stealth-testing on public inference platforms before a named launch.

Key signal: Watch whether the $0.045/task economics hold once GLM-5.3-Flash carries its own name and faces the pricing pressure that hit Zhipu shares after Kimi K3.

STORY 2 · AI & TECH / SEMICONDUCTORS
8,000 MT/s, Under 1%: China's CXMT Closes the DDR5 Gap With SK Hynix

New benchmark testing reported Aug 27 shows CXMT (长鑫存储) DDR5 memory running stably at 8,000 MT/s, within ~1% of SK Hynix's comparable M-die on mainstream platforms and 1–2% on more memory-sensitive AMD builds — the tightest published gap yet. That contrasts with mid-July overclocker testing that found CXMT dies resistant to voltage scaling, unable to tighten sub-timings, and inconsistent batch-to-batch.


CXMT priced China's largest-ever A-share semiconductor IPO in July (¥66.6B) and already supplies HP, Asus, and Acer for non-US notebook SKUs. A stock-speed benchmark match is a narrower claim than matching manufacturing yield or reliability.

Key signal: The July batch-variance and voltage-scaling findings haven't been retested — the reliability question is separate from, and unresolved by, this benchmark result.

STORY 3 · FINANCE & MACRO / INDUSTRIAL DATA
11.2% in July, Weakest All Year: China's Industrial Profit Growth Cools for a Third Month

NBS data released Aug 27 showed profits at large-scale industrial enterprises (规模以上工业企业) up 11.2% y/y in July, down from 15.1% in June — the third straight month of deceleration. Cumulative Jan–Jul profits reached ¥4.58 trillion, up 17.6% y/y, but electronics manufacturing alone (+110%) contributed 9.3 of those 17.6 percentage points.


Mining profits rose 34.9%, manufacturing 18.8%, while power/heat/gas/water fell 5.8%. The composition matters more than the headline: roughly half of year-to-date profit growth traces to one AI-hardware-linked sector.

Key signal: A durable reacceleration needs growth to broaden beyond electronics into the sectors currently flat or contracting — not just a continuation of the AI-hardware trade.
