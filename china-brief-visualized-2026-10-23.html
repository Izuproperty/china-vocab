STORY 1 · AI & TECH · CHIPS
Huawei Takes a ¥15 Billion Offshore Loan From Six Chinese Banks, Its First Offshore Syndicated Borrowing Since 2020, After First-Half Cash Flow Fell to Negative ¥39.9 Billion

Bloomberg reported the seven-year offshore yuan facility on October 22. Bank of China, CCB and ICBC are among six domestic lenders; pricing was not disclosed. Huawei talked to international banks first and closed with the domestic group.


Huawei spent ¥121B on R&D in the first half and stockpiled memory as prices rose, which took cash flow to −¥39.9B. Ascend demand exceeds supply, with DeepSeek alone planning at least 160,000 units in Inner Mongolia.

Key signal: State banks are funding Huawei's chip buildout while foreign lenders stay out, and memory stockpiling at negative cash flow means Huawei expects component costs to keep rising, so Ascend prices are unlikely to ease soon.

STORY 2 · AI & TECH · AGENTS
Former Doubao PC Lead Qi Junyuan's Today AI Passes $200 Million in Funding From Tencent, Ant Group, IDG and HSG, Seven Months After Registration

Today AI said on October 22 that cumulative funding has passed $200M from IDG, Tencent, 5Y Capital, Ant Group, HSG, Transsion, Tongge and SJTU's Hanyuan Asset. Round sizes and valuation were not disclosed. Founder Qi Junyuan built Teambition and later ran ByteDance's Doubao PC client.


The Today agent reads email, calendar, notes and files with permission and can operate a cloud computer. It launched in China on September 24 with WeCom, Baidu Netdisk, Kingsoft Docs and Tencent Meeting hooks. An early review found it useful for scheduling email but prone to unrequested suggestions.

Key signal: Tencent and Ant are both paying for a seat at the agent layer that sits above their own apps, but the startup depends on those platforms keeping access open and competes with Doubao, Qwen and Yuanbao for the same slot.

STORY 3 · FINANCE & MACRO · MARKETS
The CSI 300 Closes 20.2% Below Its June High and Enters a Bear Market, Heading for a Tenth Straight Weekly Loss as AI Hardware Stocks Unwind

The index fell 1.8% on October 22 to 4,036.30, its lowest since July 2025. Semiconductors lost 3.6% on the day, and Z.ai, MiniMax, SMIC and Alibaba each fell about 6% or more. About $2.4 trillion of market value is gone since the peak.


Q3 growth of 4.5% and a 17th month of unchanged LPR reduced the case for quick stimulus. Central Huijin has reinvested about $43B since July after selling about $231B of ETFs in the first half. The yuan sits near a four-year high, so foreign money is not fleeing.

Key signal: The drawdown is concentrated in the STAR Market chip and model names Beijing wants to finance, which raises their cost of equity and pushes them toward state funds and bank loans while regulators hold PSL and relending tools in reserve.
