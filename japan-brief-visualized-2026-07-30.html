STORY 1 · AI & TECH IT Business Today
Hitachi cuts cybersecurity verification from weeks to hours using Anthropic's Claude Mythos

Under the June 2, 2026 "Project Glasswing" collaboration, Hitachi applied a preview version of Claude Mythos to 100+ social-infrastructure cases, mostly in the energy sector, automating the creation of proof-of-concept exploit code and detecting vulnerabilities across source trees spanning millions of lines.

It's Hitachi's third disclosed Anthropic milestone this year (Lumada 3.0 in May, Glasswing in June), and lands one day before NTT Data's own Claude-based Cyber Defense Center launches on July 31 — Japan's largest integrators are racing to be the reference case, not sharing a utility.

Investor read-through: Speed gains are qualitative for now — watch whether Hitachi quantifies cost or headcount impact in coming earnings before treating this as a margin story.

STORY 2 · SEMI & INDUSTRIAL Investing.com / Seeking Alpha
Advantest posts record Q1 profit on AI tester demand, but shares slip on margin caution

Record Q1 FY2026: EPS $1.51, 33.2% net margin, ~56% ROE, driven by CPU/GPU/ASIC/networking tester demand plus FX tailwind. Management raised its SoC tester market view and said capacity expansion is being pulled forward.

Shares still fell 1.22% to ¥25,200 — the market wants proof that pricing power holds as capacity scales, not just that revenue is growing. Advantest reports first in this week's equipment gauntlet, ahead of Tokyo Electron (Jul 30) and Kioxia (Jul 31).

Investor read-through: Record fundamentals didn't stop the sell-off — Tokyo Electron and Kioxia prints are the next test of whether July 28's CXMT-triggered rout was a durable repricing or an overreaction.

STORY 3 · MACRO & BOJ Bloomberg / Reuters
Yen near a 40-year low, JGB yields near record as the BOJ opens its two-day policy meeting

USD/JPY traded near ¥163.7 on July 29, its worst week since May, as the BOJ opens its July 30–31 meeting. Consensus expects a hold at 1.00% with an FY2026 growth upgrade toward ~0.8%. Separately, the 40-year JGB yield climbed to 4.01% on July 24, nearing its May record of 4.355%.

Finance Minister Katayama's verbal-intervention warnings have done little to deter yen sellers. The decision itself is largely priced — Ueda's Friday 3:30pm press-conference language is the actual event to watch.

Investor read-through: Hawkish language without a hike would likely support the yen; cautious language against this week's chip-sector volatility would read as the BOJ prioritizing financial stability over currency defense.
